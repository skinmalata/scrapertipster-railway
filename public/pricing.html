Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the Pro plan?

Every plan includes the exact same features — all prediction categories (1X2, Over/Under, BTTS, Corners, Cards, streaks and more), daily Best Picks and H2H Picks, Highest Scoring Half, the unlimited Ticket Builder, live in-play tips and match stats. Paid plans add the exclusive VIP Tips section: match-winner record certs on teams with near-perfect form and a clean head-to-head record, plus team-to-score calls from our xG model.

Is the Ticket Builder free?

Yes. The Ticket Builder is completely free and unlimited for every user — no sign-up, no subscription, no limits. Build accumulators and bet builders with up to 30 legs and 500 combined odds.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a refund policy?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all plans. Contact our support team for assistance.

How do payments work?

Payments are processed securely through Whop or PayPal. Whop subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled; PayPal is a one-time payment for the chosen period (renew manually when it ends).

Do I need an account for free content?

No. Free daily picks, Highest Scoring Half, 2 Odds of the Day and the Ticket Builder all work without signing up. Accounts are created when you upgrade to Pro — you pay first, then your account is set up automatically.
