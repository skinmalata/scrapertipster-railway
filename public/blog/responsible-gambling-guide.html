Understanding the Risks

Gambling involves risk. Understanding these risks is the first step to staying safe:

  • Financial loss: You can lose money - there's no guaranteed win
  • Addiction: Gambling can become addictive for some people
  • Chasing losses: Trying to win back lost money leads to bigger losses
  • Impact on life: Problem gambling affects relationships, work, and mental health

Principles of Responsible Gambling

âœ… What Responsible Gambling Looks Like

  • Viewing it as entertainment: Not a way to make money
  • Only betting what you can afford to lose: Using disposable income
  • Setting time limits: Not spending hours gambling
  • Taking breaks: Not gambling every day
  • Keeping it separate: Not mixing gambling with other activities
  • Knowing when to stop: Walking away after a loss

Warning Signs of Problem Gambling

Be aware of these warning signs in yourself and others:

ðŸš© Red Flags

  • Spending more money than you can afford
  • Feeling anxious when not gambling
  • Lying about gambling habits
  • Chasing losses
  • Neglecting responsibilities for gambling
  • Betting to escape problems
  • Borrowing money to gamble

Practical Protection Measures

1. Set a Budget

Decide how much you can afford to lose before you start. Never exceed this amount.

2. Set Time Limits

Decide how long you'll gamble. Set alarms to remind yourself.

3. Never Chase Losses

Accepting a loss is part of gambling. Trying to win back losses usually leads to bigger problems.

4. Take Regular Breaks

Step away from gambling regularly. Clear your head and reassess.

5. Don't Gamble When Upset

Emotional states impair judgment. Avoid gambling when angry, stressed, or depressed.

6. Use Tools Available

Most betting sites offer deposit limits, time limits, and self-exclusion options. Use them.

Treat the money you gamble as gone the moment you place the bet. Any win is a bonus. This mindset prevents chasing losses and keeps gambling as entertainment.

Getting Help

If you or someone you know is struggling with gambling, help is available. These organizations offer free, confidential support:

  • GamCare: 0808 8020 133 (UK)
  • Gamblers Anonymous: www.gamblersanonymous.org
  • BeGambleAware: www.begambleaware.org
