Football is the heartbeat of Cameroonian betting, and mobile money is the wallet. Around 90% of deposits and withdrawals run through MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money, and the market has boomed accordingly — betting shops and online platforms now compete over everything from the domestic Elite One to the Premier League, La Liga and Ligue 1, with MMA arriving on the back of UFC heavyweight champion Francis Ngannou's fame.

Legally, Cameroon is stricter than many African markets. Online betting is regulated by the Ministry of Territorial Administration (MINAT) under Law 2015/012 and Decree 2019/2300/PM. Legitimate online operators must hold a MINAT authorisation, be locally incorporated, run .cm domains, and route all payments through the state-designated aggregator InTouch Cameroun (mandatory since 30 January 2025).

This guide ranks the best licensed betting sites in Cameroon for 2026 on their MINAT licence standing, MTN and Orange Money support, Elite One coverage and withdrawal speed.

Know the Rules: MINAT Authorisation and the InTouch Switch

Online operators in Cameroon need a MINAT authorisation, a locally incorporated Cameroonian company (Sarl or SA) and a .cm domain. Verify any site by scanning the footer for a licence number in the format n XXXXXX/A/MINAT/SG/DAP/SDLP/SJ and matching the named entity to the company on your deposit receipt. There is no single public registry, so the operator's own published footer is the primary check.

On 30 January 2025 MINAT enforced an exclusive payments contract with InTouch Cameroun. Deposits that had been paid straight from MTN MoMo or Orange Money to operator wallets were briefly blocked, and brands like betPawa, 1xBet, Betwinner and Betmomo had to re-route; most restored service within days. Since then, all legal betting payments flow through InTouch. From 1 January 2026, offshore operators serving Cameroonian players without local authorisation also face a 3% turnover tax and growing enforcement scrutiny.

Licence check: betPawa (MINAT 000975 via Ennovative Gaming Sarl), 1xBet (MINAT 000020, issued 24 February 2023) and Paripesa (MINAT 000347 via PRESTAS-SOLUTIONS SARL) are among the major brands publishing valid MINAT licence numbers. Verify the entity name before depositing.

Responsible Gambling in Cameroon

The 2015 law and its decree require licensed operators to offer self-exclusion and responsible-gambling information, and you can escalate unresolved complaints to MINAT in writing. Set deposit limits before you start, treat betting as entertainment, and never stake money you cannot afford to lose — the 18+ age rule applies to betting, with casinos stricter at 21+.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is online betting legal in Cameroon?

Yes, for operators holding a MINAT authorisation: they must be locally incorporated, run a .cm domain, and route all payments through the InTouch Cameroun aggregator. Offshore operators without local authorisation face a 3% turnover tax from 2026 and enforcement scrutiny.

Which betting sites accept Orange Money and MTN Mobile Money in Cameroon?

Virtually all licensed operators do — Premier Bet, betPawa, BettoMax, 1xBet, Betwinner and Paripesa, among others. MTN Mobile Money and Orange Money cover around 90% of the market and payouts typically land within minutes to a few hours.

What is the legal betting age in Cameroon?

18+ for sports betting: the 2019 decree bars participation by anyone who has not reached the age of civil majority. Casino venues commonly enforce a stricter 21+ policy.

Are betting winnings taxed in Cameroon?

No player-side withholding on sports-betting winnings is identified under Cameroonian tax law; operator-side taxes apply (15% GGR plus 10% supplementary for casinos; a 3% turnover tax on offshore operators from January 2026). Since January 2026 a 1% plus FCFA 4 fee applies to gambling-related electronic transfers.

Why did my MTN or Orange deposit fail in early 2025?

On 30 January 2025 MINAT enforced an exclusive payments contract with InTouch Cameroun, so operators had to re-route direct mobile-money deposits through the aggregator. Most brands, including betPawa, 1xBet and Betwinner, restored service within days.
