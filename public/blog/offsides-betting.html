Offsides Betting: Niche Market Opportunity

Updated April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team | 8 min read

Offsides betting is very niche but offers value. Bet on over/under offsides for teams. Less liquid but potentially profitable.

Offsides Strategy

  • High-pressing teams concede more offsides
  • Target teams with technical attackers
  • Check average offsides per game
💡 Tip: Use StatsBomb or Opta for offsides data.
