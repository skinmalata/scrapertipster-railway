Flat Betting vs Variable Staking: Which Bankroll Strategy Wins?

Updated April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team | 13 min read

Your staking strategy matters more than your selection method. Most bettors focus on picking winners, but how much you bet determines whether you profit long-term. Let's compare the two main approaches.

What is Flat Betting?

You bet the same amount on every bet, regardless of confidence or odds.

Stake = Bankroll × 1-2% (always the same)
  • Simple to follow
  • Predictable bankroll swings
  • No complex calculations
  • Psychologically easy

What is Variable Staking?

You adjust your stake based on confidence, odds, or edge size.

Stake = f(confidence, odds, Kelly %)
  • Maximizes value on confident bets
  • Can grow bankroll faster
  • Requires probability estimates
  • More complex to manage

Variable Staking Methods

1. Percentage of Bankroll

Stake = Bankroll % (changes as bankroll changes)

Example: Always 2% of current bankroll

2. Kelly Criterion

Stake = Kelly % × (see our Kelly guide)

Most mathematically optimal but complex

3. Confidence-Based

Stake = 1 unit for low, 3 units for high confidence

Requires honest self-assessment

4. Odds-Based

Stake = Higher for lower odds, lower for higher odds

Often used by professional bettors

💡 Recommendation: Start with flat betting. Once you have proven probability estimation skills, transition to variable staking.

When Flat Betting Wins

  • You're still learning to find value
  • You don't have reliable probability estimates
  • You want to minimize variance
  • You bet infrequently
  • You struggle with emotional betting

When Variable Staking Wins

  • You have proven edge (55%+ accuracy)
  • You're betting 20+ times per week
  • You can estimate probabilities accurately
  • You want to maximize bankroll growth
  • You can handle variance emotionally
⚠️ Variable Staking Warning: If your probability estimates are wrong, variable staking amplifies losses faster than flat betting.

The Hybrid Approach

Many pros use a hybrid:

  • Base flat stake (e.g., 1% of bankroll)
  • Multiplier for confidence (0.5x to 2x)
  • Maximum stake cap (never more than 3% on any bet)

Start with flat betting at Bet365, then transition to Kelly Criterion once you have a proven edge.

Verdict: Which Wins?

For beginners: Flat betting wins. For experienced bettors with proven edges: Variable wins.

  • ✅ Beginners → Flat betting (simpler, less variance)
  • ✅ Intermediate → Hybrid approach
  • ✅ Advanced → Kelly Criterion variable staking
  • ⚠️ Never variable stake without accurate probability estimates

The best staking strategy is one you can follow consistently with discipline.
