2. Welcome Bonus & Promotions Winner: 1xBet

1xBet dominates the bonus category. New Nigerian users get a 300% first deposit bonus up to ₦1,200,000 — the largest welcome offer from any bookmaker operating in the country. The wagering requirements are standard at 5x on accumulator bets with minimum odds of 1.40. Beyond the welcome bonus, 1xBet runs weekly cashback offers, accumulator boosts, and free bet promotions that keep existing customers engaged.

Betway offers a 100% deposit match up to ₦100,000, which is modest by comparison but comes with simpler wagering terms (5x at odds of 1.50). Betway also runs regular promotions including free-to-play prediction games and odds boosts on major events. While the raw numbers favour 1xBet, Betway's simpler terms mean you are more likely to actually clear the bonus.

4. Mobile App Experience Winner: Betway

Betway wins the mobile category with a cleaner, faster, and more intuitive app. Available on both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store, Betway's app is well-optimised for Nigerian phone networks and features a streamlined bet slip, fast live betting updates, and a modern dark theme that is easy on the eyes during long evening betting sessions.

1xBet's app is feature-packed but cluttered. The sheer number of markets, promotions, and options crammed into the interface can be overwhelming for new users. Navigation is not always intuitive, and the app can feel sluggish on older phones. However, power users who want access to every possible market and feature may prefer 1xBet's all-in-one approach.

5. Payment Methods & Withdrawal Speed Winner: 1xBet

1xBet is the clear winner here. With over 200 payment methods including Bitcoin, USDT, Opay, Palmpay, USSD, bank transfer, and dozens of e-wallets, 1xBet offers the most flexible payment experience of any bookmaker in Nigeria. Crypto users especially benefit — deposits are instant and withdrawals typically arrive within 15 minutes.

Betway supports the core Nigerian payment methods — bank transfers, debit cards, and USSD — but lacks the breadth of options that 1xBet provides. There is no crypto support, no Palmpay integration, and fewer e-wallet options. Withdrawal speeds are comparable at 1-24 hours for bank transfers.

Important Note

If you plan to use cryptocurrency for betting, 1xBet is one of the few Nigerian-licensed bookmakers that supports Bitcoin, Ethereum, and USDT deposits and withdrawals.

6. NPFL Coverage Winner: Draw

Neither 1xBet nor Betway is the best choice for Nigerian Premier Football League (NPFL) coverage. Both offer limited markets on NPFL matches compared to Bet9ja, which dominates local league coverage with 80+ markets per match. 1xBet and Betway both cover around 20-30 markets per NPFL fixture, which is sufficient for casual bettors but falls short for serious Nigerian league specialists.

If NPFL betting is a priority for you, Bet9ja remains the best option for local league depth and market variety.

7. Live Betting & Streaming Winner: 1xBet

1xBet offers free live streaming on over 1,000 events daily, including football, tennis, basketball, and esports. The streaming is available directly within the app and website without requiring a funded account — you just need to be logged in. This is one of 1xBet's biggest selling points and a feature that no other Nigerian bookmaker matches at this scale.

Betway offers live streaming on select events, but the coverage is much more limited. Their live betting platform is solid with fast odds updates and a good range of in-play markets, but the lack of comprehensive free streaming puts them at a disadvantage compared to 1xBet.

8. Trust, Licensing & Regulation Winner: Betway

Betway wins the trust category decisively. Parent company Entain plc is listed on the London Stock Exchange and operates under strict UK Gambling Commission regulations. In Nigeria, Betway holds a valid NLRC licence and has built a strong reputation for fair play, timely payouts, and responsible gambling practices. Entain's public-company status means financial transparency and accountability that privately-held operators cannot match.

1xBet holds an NLRC licence for Nigeria and a Curaçao e-gaming licence globally. While 1xBet has improved its regulatory compliance in recent years, the brand has faced regulatory challenges in several markets. For bettors who prioritise operating behind a publicly-listed, heavily-regulated parent company, Betway offers significantly more peace of mind.

9. Customer Support Winner: Betway

Betway offers better overall customer support with responsive live chat, email support, and a comprehensive help centre. Their responsible gambling tools are also superior — including deposit limits, loss limits, session reminders, and self-exclusion options that are easy to access and configure. This reflects Entain's commitment to responsible gambling as a publicly-listed operator.

1xBet offers 24/7 live chat and email support, which is generally responsive but can be inconsistent during peak hours. Their responsible gambling tools exist but are less prominent and harder to find within the app compared to Betway's approach.
