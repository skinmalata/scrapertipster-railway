Throw-Ins Betting: Niche Statistical Market

Updated April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team | 7 min read

Throw-ins betting is extremely niche. Some bookmakers offer over/under throw-ins. Very hard to predict but exists.

Throw-Ins Strategy

  • Wing-heavy teams get more throw-ins
  • High-press teams concede throw-ins
  • Check average throw-ins per match
