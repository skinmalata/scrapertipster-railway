BTTS Betting Guide: Both Teams to Score Explained

Last updated: April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team

Tip: Looking for today's football predictions? Visit the homepage.

Both Teams to Score (BTTS) is one of the simplest and most enjoyable markets in football betting. You're simply backing whether both teams will find the net during the match.

No need to predict the exact score or winner - just whether both sides get on the scoresheet. It's perfect for matches where you're unsure who will win but confident both teams will score.

How Does BTTS Work?

You have two options:

  • BTTS Yes - Both teams score at least once
  • BTTS No - At least one team fails to score

That's it. 2-1, 3-1, 1-1 - any result where both teams score is a winner for BTTS Yes.

Real Example

Chelsea vs Arsenal

BTTS Yes: 1.65

BTTS No: 2.20

You bet £10 on BTTS Yes. Final score: 2-2. You win £16.50.

If it ends 1-0 to Chelsea, you lose.

The Eredivisie averages 68% BTTS hits - that's nearly 7 out of 10 matches. If you want consistent BTTS bets, focus on Dutch football. The odds will be lower, but the hit rate is much higher.

When to Back BTTS

Certain matchups are more likely to produce BTTS than others:

Best Spots for BTTS Yes

  • Attacking teams vs weak defences - One team scores, other gets beaten but still scores
  • Derby matches - Pride often leads to open games
  • Both teams in good scoring form - Look at last 5 matches for both
  • Second half of season - Teams have less to hide, more attacking
  • Noon kick-offs - Early games tend to be more open

Perfect BTTS Scenario

Imagine two in-form attacking teams, both missing their first-choice centre-backs, playing in good weather at 12pm. This is your ideal BTTS candidate.

When to Avoid BTTS

⚠️ Avoid in These Situations
  • Must-win games - Teams play cautiously, focus on not conceding
  • Bottom of the table clashes - Both playing not to lose
  • Cold/rainy conditions - Goals harder to come by
  • Strong defensive teams - Even if they concede, they don't score much
  • Key striker missing - Dramatically reduces scoring chances

Combining BTTS with Other Markets

BTTS becomes even more powerful when combined with other markets:

Combo BetDescriptionWhy It Works

BTTS Yes + DrawBoth score and match ends levelOften offers value
BTTS + Double ChanceBTTS Yes + 1X or X2More chance to win

BTTS + Over 2.5 Combo

This is one of my favourite acca builders. When you find a match likely to produce goals, combining these two markets gives you much better odds than either alone.

A typical Premier League match might be:

  • BTTS Yes: 1.75
  • Over 2.5: 1.80
  • Combined: around 2.50-2.75

Key Factors for BTTS

Check This Before Betting

  1. xG for both teams - Are they creating enough chances?
  2. Goalsconceded - Do they keep clean sheets?
  3. Last 5 matches BTTS rate - Form is key
  4. Head-to-head - Some matches always produce goals
  5. Team news - Missing attackers or defenders?
  6. Weather - Rain and cold kill goals

BTTS in Play (In-Play Betting)

BTTS becomes really interesting in-play. Here are some scenarios:

  • 0-0 at halftime, both having chances - Good value for BTTS Yes
  • 1-0 at 70 minutes, equal game - Second goal likely
  • 2-0 late game, one team parking bus - Might still get one

Live BTTS betting requires watching the game, which adds another dimension to your betting.
