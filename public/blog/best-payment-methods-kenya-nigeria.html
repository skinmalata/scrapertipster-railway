Best Payment Methods for Kenyan and Nigerian Bettors

Updated: 2026 | 8 min read

Tip: Looking for today's football predictions? Click here.

Choosing the right payment method for betting in Kenya and Nigeria can mean the difference between depositing instantly with no fees or waiting days for a withdrawal. Each method has trade-offs around speed, cost, limits, and accessibility. This guide breaks down every major option available to Kenyan and Nigerian bettors on platforms like Betway, Bet9ja, 22Bet, and others.

Kenyan Payment Methods

1. M-Pesa (Safaricom)

Kenya's dominant mobile money service. Betway Kenya, 22Bet Kenya, and most local bookmakers support M-Pesa natively. Deposits are instant; withdrawals take 2–24 hours.

Best for: Deposits under KES 70,000 (daily M-Pesa limit). Instant access to betting funds.

2. Airtel Money

A viable alternative to M-Pesa with lower transaction limits. Some bookmakers offer exclusive Airtel Money promotions. Widely accepted but less universal than M-Pesa.

3. Cards (Visa/Mastercard)

Available on international-facing sites. Requires a Kenyan Shilling account or USD card. May incur foreign exchange fees. Best for depositing on sites without M-Pesa support.

Nigerian Payment Methods

1. Direct Bank Transfer (Naira)

The most popular method for Nigerian bettors. Bet9ja, Nairabet, and 22Bet Nigeria all offer dedicated Naira accounts. Transfers from GTBank, First Bank, and Access Bank are processed fastest.

Best for: Large deposits above NGN 100,000 where bank-to-bank transfer is free.

2. USSD Transfer

Dial *737# (GTBank) or your bank's USSD code to transfer without internet. Works on basic phones. Slightly slower than app transfers but universally accessible.

3. Opay / PalmPay

Digital banks and mobile wallets growing rapidly in Nigeria. Opay and PalmPay are accepted by some betting platforms for instant deposits.

Cross-Border: Cryptocurrency

Both Kenyan and Nigerian bettors increasingly use USDT (Tether) and Bitcoin for betting, especially on international platforms. Stake and 1xBet support crypto deposits.

Advantages: No bank involvement, instant transfers, no currency conversion, accessible globally. Disadvantages: Price volatility, requires crypto knowledge, not regulated.

Pro Tip: Use the Same Method for Deposit and Withdrawal

Most betting sites require withdrawals to go back to the original deposit method (anti-money laundering compliance). If you deposited via M-Pesa, withdraw via M-Pesa. If via bank transfer, use the same bank. This prevents account freezes and verification delays. Betway and Bet365 enforce this rule consistently.

Fee Comparison by Method

Lowest Fees by Use Case

Small deposits (under KES 1,500 / NGN 50,000): M-Pesa (Kenya) or USSD (Nigeria) — minimal fees, instant processing.

Large deposits: Bank transfer (both countries) — usually free on betting sites, though your bank may charge a transfer fee.

Frequent small bettors: Digital wallets (Opay, Airtel Money) — often fee-free for small frequent transactions.

International platforms: Cryptocurrency (USDT/BTC) — network fees only, avoids FX charges entirely.

The Bottom Line

For Kenyan bettors, M-Pesa is unbeatable for convenience and speed. For Nigerian bettors, direct bank transfers serve large deposits well while USSD covers quick plays. If you bet on international platforms, consider USDT to avoid banking complications entirely.

Bet Responsibly

Use deposit limits and only bet with money you can afford to lose. For support, visit GamCare.
