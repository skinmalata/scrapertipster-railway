Goal Scoring Markets: Anytime, First, Last Scorer Guide

Updated April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team | 10 min read

Goal scorer betting is huge. Anytime scorer is most popular, but first/last offer bigger odds. Here's how to profit from scorer markets.

Types of Scorer Bets

  • Anytime scorer - Player scores at any point
  • First scorer - Player scores first goal
  • Last scorer - Player scores last goal
  • 2+ goals - Player scores 2 or more
💡 Tip: Anytime scorer has best odds-to-chance ratio for most matches.
