Corner Betting Strategy: Best Markets and Tips

Updated April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team | 11 min read

Corner betting is an often-overlooked market with great value. Learn which corners markets offer the best opportunities.

Best Corner Markets

  • Over/Under 8.5, 9.5, 10.5 corners
  • Asian Handicap corners
  • First corner timing
  • Team corners totals
💡 Tip: Teams with high possession and wing play generate more corners.

Best Leagues for Corners

Premier League, Champions League, and Bundesliga have highest corner counts. Serie A lower.

Bet365 and Betfair offer best corner markets and in-play options.
