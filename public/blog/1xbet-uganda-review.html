1xBet Uganda: Licensing and Legal Status

1xBet is licensed and regulated in Uganda by the National Lotteries and Gaming Regulatory Board (NLGRB), operating under its Curaçao master license. The NLGRB was established under the National Lotteries and Gaming Act, 2016 and is responsible for licensing, regulating, and monitoring all gaming activities in Uganda. The NLGRB operates under the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

The NLGRB license requires operators to comply with Ugandan gambling laws, including responsible gambling measures, player fund protection, tax compliance, and anti-money laundering protocols. Licensed operators must meet strict capital requirements and pay annual license fees. 1xBet's NLGRB authorization confirms that the operator has met these requirements and is legally authorized to offer betting services to Ugandan residents.

For Ugandan bettors, using an NLGRB-licensed operator provides regulatory protection that offshore-only platforms cannot offer. The NLGRB handles player complaints and can intervene in disputes between bettors and licensed operators. The board has been increasingly active in enforcing compliance, including shutting down unlicensed operators and strengthening consumer protections.

Important: Uganda's 25+ Legal Betting Age

Uganda is one of the few countries in Africa where the minimum legal gambling age is 25 years, not 18. This applies to all forms of gambling, including sports betting.
