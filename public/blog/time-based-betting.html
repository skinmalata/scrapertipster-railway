Key Factors That Influence Goal Timing

1. Team Pace of Play

High-pressing teams score earlier on average. Teams that build up play slowly see more second-half goals. Check a team's average first goal time on SofaScore.

2. Defensive Quality vs Attacking Intent

A match between two defensive teams is likely to stay goalless early. The longer a 0-0 draw continues, the more both teams open up â€” shifting probability toward the final 30 minutes.

3. Match Context

Must-win matches see earlier goals. Cup finals and relegation battles historically produce earlier goals than mid-table fixtures.

4. Weather

Heavy rain slows games, reduces early goal attempts, and pushes goals later. Check the forecast before betting on early time markets.
