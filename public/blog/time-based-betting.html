Time-Based Betting: Wagering on Goals in Specific Minutes

Updated: 2026 | 9 min read

Tip: Looking for in-play football predictions? Click here.

Among the most nuanced betting markets in football are time-based bets — wagers tied to when something happens rather than if it happens. These markets reward bettors who understand the temporal patterns of football matches and are offered by Bet365, Betway, and 1xBet.

What Are Time-Based Betting Markets?

Time-based betting covers any market where timing is the primary condition. Examples include:

  • First goal before/after a specific minute
  • Goal in a specific 15-minute interval (e.g., 15:00–30:00)
  • Last goal before half-time
  • Goal in the last 10 minutes of a match
  • Goal in the 76–90 minute window
Example — "Goal in 15:01–30:00":

You bet that a goal will be scored between 15 and 30 minutes. The odds might be 2.50.

If the first goal comes at 22' — you win. If it's 8' or 33' — you lose.
Goals after 30' don't affect this bet; only the FIRST goal matters.

How Bookmakers Set Time-Based Lines

Bookmakers use aggregate data from thousands of matches. Research shows that goal distribution follows a U-shaped curve — more goals are scored early and late, with fewer in the middle of each half.

Key Factors That Influence Goal Timing

1. Team Pace of Play

High-pressing teams score earlier on average. Teams that build up play slowly see more second-half goals. Check a team's average first goal time on SofaScore.

2. Defensive Quality vs Attacking Intent

A match between two defensive teams is likely to stay goalless early. The longer a 0-0 draw continues, the more both teams open up — shifting probability toward the final 30 minutes.

3. Match Context

Must-win matches see earlier goals. Cup finals and relegation battles historically produce earlier goals than mid-table fixtures.

4. Weather

Heavy rain slows games, reduces early goal attempts, and pushes goals later. Check the forecast before betting on early time markets.

The In-Play Time Correction Strategy

If no goal is scored in the first 20 minutes and both teams are creating chances, the"Goal before 30:00" market on Bet365 often becomes great value. At 20 minutes with a blank scoreline but active play, the market hasn't caught up — back it at inflated odds.

Pro Tip: Check First-Goal Average Times

FBref provides detailed timing stats. A team averaging 28 minutes for their first goal has a different profile than one averaging 44 minutes. Use this to find edges in first-goal minute markets.

Popular Time Betting Strategies

Late Goal Backing (76+ Minutes)

Matches that are goalless or low-scoring at 70 minutes but where one team needs a goal — back the"Goal in 76–90" market. The desperation in the final stretch creates value.

First 15 Minutes

When a high-pressing team faces a slow-starting opponent, the first 15 minutes offer the highest probability window. Back"Goal in 0–15" on Betway when teams have this profile.

Beware added time: The 90th minute is often closer to the 93rd in modern football. Time markets end at 90:00 on some bookmakers and 90:05 on others. Always check the market rules.

The Bottom Line

Time-based betting rewards bettors who understand goal distribution patterns, team tempo tendencies, and match context. Use first-goal average data from FBref and SofaScore, and look for in-play opportunities where the market hasn't caught up to the actual play.
