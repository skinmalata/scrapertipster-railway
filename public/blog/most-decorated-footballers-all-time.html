The Most Decorated Footballers of All Time

Medals are the true measure of a career. From Lionel Messi's record haul to the African and European legends chasing him, here are the most decorated footballers in history.

Individual awards tell one story. Trophies tell another. Winning a league title takes a full season of consistency; winning a cup takes knockout nerves; winning a World Cup takes a four-year peak. The players who stacked up all of them, season after season, are the most decorated footballers in history — and the list is a who's who of the game's great dynasties.

Messi Extends the Record

As of the 2026 summer, Lionel Messi sits alone at the top of the all-time list with 46 senior career trophies. That total counts every major competition he won with Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain and Inter Miami, plus the 2022 World Cup and Copa America titles with Argentina. It is the largest haul any footballer has ever collected.

Counting caveat: Trophy counts depend on what is included. Some rankings count only 'senior' honours, others add youth and underage titles, which is why you will see Messi quoted anywhere from 44 to 48. The universally accepted senior figure is 46.

How the Barcelona Core Stacked Them Up

Between 2008 and 2015, Barcelona won everything available, and the same players collected medals for all of it. Dani Alves won 43 trophies across Brazil, Spain and Italy, spending nine trophy-laden years at Barcelona. Andres Iniesta, Gerard Pique and Lionel Messi each won ten La Liga titles, four Champions Leagues and countless cups, and then added the 2010 World Cup and Euro 2008 and 2012 with Spain.

The African Giants: Hossam Hassan and Hossam Ashour

It is easy to forget that the all-time list is not purely European. Hossam Hassan, the Egyptian striker, won 41 trophies in a 30-year career spanning Al Ahly, Zamalek and the Egyptian national team. His countryman Hossam Ashour collected 39, almost all of them with Al Ahly, whose dominance of the CAF Champions League produced a medal haul no European defender could match.

Why the Record May Never Move Again

To pass Messi, a player born in the 2020s would need to win roughly four trophies a year for twelve years straight — and that is before they count. More realistically, the modern Champions League format and expanded domestic calendars make deep trophy hauls slightly harder, and the dominance of one or two clubs means medals are concentrated among fewer players.

The most decorated footballer in history is, for now, comfortably Lionel Messi. For the full story of Egypt's decorated captain, see our dedicated profile of Hossam Ashour.
