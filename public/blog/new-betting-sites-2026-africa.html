Frequently Asked Questions

Are new betting sites safe to use in Nigeria?
Yes, as long as they are properly licensed. BetKing is LSLB-licensed in Nigeria. All the sites we recommend are either licensed by Nigerian or Kenyan regulators or hold reputable international licenses. Always check the licensing information on the site footer before depositing.
Which new betting site has the best welcome bonus?
PariPesa offers the highest value welcome bonus at 200% up to ₦150,000 when depositing with cryptocurrency. Melbet offers 200% up to ₦100,000 for standard deposits.
Do any new betting sites accept M-Pesa in Kenya?
Yes. BangBet and 22Bet both accept M-Pesa deposits in Kenya. They also support Airtel Money and bank transfers.
Which new betting site has the best mobile app?
22Bet and BangBet have the best mobile apps among new betting sites. Melbet and PariPesa also offer solid Android apps. None of the new sites currently offer native iOS apps.
Should I switch from Bet9ja to a new betting site?
Not necessarily — Bet9ja is still the market leader for a reason. But opening an account at a new betting site can be worthwhile for the welcome bonus. Many experienced Nigerian bettors maintain accounts at Bet9ja for daily betting and use new sites for their sign-up bonuses.
