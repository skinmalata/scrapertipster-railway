Frequently Asked Questions

Which betting site has the best enhanced odds in Nigeria?
Betway offers the best daily enhanced odds in Nigeria with consistent 3-5 boosted markets and up to 100% extra value. Bet9ja's Super Boost offers higher percentages (up to 200%) but less frequently.
Which betting site has the best enhanced odds in Kenya?
1xBet offers the most boosted markets in Kenya (10-15 daily), while Betway offers better boost percentages. Betika's Bet of the Day is a good option for simple single-match boosts.
What is the difference between enhanced odds and a welcome bonus?
Enhanced odds (or price boosts) improve the odds on specific markets for a limited time. Welcome bonuses are deposit-based offers for new customers. Enhanced odds are available to existing customers, while welcome bonuses are one-time offers.
Are there stake limits on boosted odds?
Yes. Most betting sites cap the maximum stake on boosted odds, typically between ₦500 and ₦5,000 depending on the site and the promotion. Betway has the highest max stake limits at ₦2,000-₦5,000.
How often do betting sites offer price boosts?
1xBet offers the most frequent boosts with 10-15 daily. Betway offers 3-5 daily. SportyBet, Bet9ja, and Betika typically offer 1-3 boosts per day, with additional special promotions on major match days.
