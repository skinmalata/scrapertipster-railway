Fouls Betting: Discipline Markets Guide

Updated April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team | 8 min read

Fouls betting lets you bet on total fouls or bookings. Over/under fouls markets exist for matches. Good for in-play.

Fouls Strategy

  • Physical teams = more fouls
  • Derby games = high foul count
  • Use booking points (yellow=10, red=25)
💡 Tip: Check referee history for leniency.
