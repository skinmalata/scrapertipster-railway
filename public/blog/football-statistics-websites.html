Why Football Statistics Matter for Betting

Relying on the"eye test" or recent results alone is a losing strategy. Football has high variance — a team can play poorly and win 1-0, or dominate and lose 2-1. Statistics cut through the noise to reveal underlying performance.

3-5%Edge possible with data-driven approach

Key Insight: Expected Goals (xG) is the single most predictive metric in football analytics. Teams consistently outperforming their xG will regress to the mean. Teams underperforming their xG represent value betting opportunities.

The websites below are the tools that give you access to this data. Each one serves a different purpose in a bettor's research workflow.

Best Free Football Statistics Websites

1. FBref

fbref.com Free StatsBomb Data

FBref is the deepest free advanced-statistics archive on the public web. Powered by StatsBomb data, it covers 40+ leagues with metrics that go far beyond basic goals and assists.

Key Data: xG, xA (expected assists), progressive passes, progressive carries, shot-creating actions, pressures, pass completion %, tackle %, aerial win %, and match-by-match player logs going back 20+ years for major leagues.

Best For: Statistical modelling, historical comparisons, deep tactical research.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Go to league tables → sort by xG difference to find over/under-performing teams
  • Compare actual points vs expected points (xGD) → teams with negative xGD but positive results are due for regression
  • Use player scouting reports to identify form players for goalscorer markets
  • Check recent match xG breakdowns to assess form beyond results

Limitations: Dated interface, no live data, no odds integration, mobile experience is poor.

2. Understat

understat.com Free xG Specialist

Understat offers the clearest xG visualisation on the web. While it only covers the top 5 European leagues (Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1) plus the Russian Premier League, the depth of presentation is unmatched.

Key Features: xG shot maps for every match, xPoints tables comparing actual vs expected standings, team xG trend graphs over a season, player xG contribution breakdowns, and shot location heatmaps.

Best For: xG-focused betting strategies on top leagues.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Check xPoints table → teams significantly above xPoints will likely regress
  • Review shot maps to see if a team creates high-quality chances or just lots of low-xG shots
  • Use the trend graphs to see if form is genuinely improving or just results-driven

Limitations: Only 5 leagues, no betting odds, no player prop data.

3. WhoScored

whoscored.com Free Match Analysis

WhoScored is the most well-rounded football statistics website for match previews and post-match analysis. It covers 50+ leagues with proprietary player ratings, detailed team statistics, and live match data.

Key Features: Player ratings (proprietary algorithm), shot-position visualisations split by zone, live match stats, team strengths & weaknesses profiles, head-to-head analysis, and predicted lineups.

Best For: Pre-match research, team style analysis, live betting context.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Read team"strengths & weaknesses" cards to understand playing style
  • Check shot position zones → teams shooting mostly from outside the box are due for regression
  • Use player ratings to track form over recent matches
  • Analyse scoring patterns (first half vs second half goals) for time-based markets

Limitations: Heavy advertising on free tier, proprietary rating system is a black box, some features require subscription.

4. SofaScore

sofascore.com Free Best Mobile App

SofaScore has the best mobile experience of any football statistics platform. Its live match centre includes Attack Momentum charts, real-time xG, heatmaps, shot maps, and predicted lineups that update as team news drops.

Key Features: Real-time xG during matches, momentum graphs, attack/defence ratings, heatmaps, player average ratings, lineup predictions with injury context, and custom notifications for teams and leagues.

Best For: Mobile-first users, live betting, and match-day research.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Set up notifications for team lineups → early team news gives an edge before odds adjust
  • Monitor live xG momentum during matches for in-play betting
  • Check pre-match head-to-head with recent form context

Limitations: Less depth than FBref for advanced metrics, some premium features require subscription.

5. Transfermarkt

transfermarkt.com Free Market Values & Injuries

Transfermarkt is the definitive public database for player market values, transfer histories, contract information, and injury archives. While it does not offer advanced match metrics, its squad and injury data is invaluable.

Key Features: Player market values updated regularly, complete transfer histories, contract expiry dates, detailed injury logs (player, injury type, return date), squad depth views, and salary estimates.

Best For: Injury research, squad rotation analysis, transfer market context.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Check injury archive before betting on player props → players with repeated muscle injuries are high risk
  • Use squad depth views to assess rotation risk during fixture congestion
  • Track market value changes → clubs selling key players often underperform in the following window

Limitations: Slow, ad-heavy interface, no xG or advanced metrics.

6. Soccerway

soccerway.com Free Fixtures & Results

Soccerway is a comprehensive results and fixtures database covering hundreds of leagues worldwide. Its clean interface makes finding historical results, head-to-head records, and league tables quick and easy.

Key Features: Complete fixture database across hundreds of leagues, head-to-head records (last 10 meetings), form tables (last 6 matches), home/away table splits, and domestic cup competition results.

Best For: Fixture planning, H2H research, league comparison.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Plan weekly betting by reviewing all league fixtures
  • Check H2H records for derby matches and tactical mismatches
  • Analyse home/away form splits → some teams are radically different at home vs away

Limitations: No advanced metrics, no xG, no odds, dated interface.

7. FootyStats

footystats.org Free Betting Hit Rates

FootyStats is built specifically for betting research. It publishes team-level hit-rate tables for over/under goals, BTTS, and corners across 2,000+ leagues. Pre-match predictions are provided for most markets.

Key Features: Over/under goals hit-rate tables, BTTS percentage rankings, corner stats, card stats, league comparison tools, and pre-match predictions.

Best For: Goal market research, BTTS betting, corner betting.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Find teams with 65%+ BTTS frequency for consistent BTTS bets
  • Identify teams averaging 7+ corners per match for corner markets
  • Use over/under trends to find high-probability goal line bets

Limitations: Player-level data is shallow, some features behind paywall, no odds compared across bookmakers.

8. Flashscore

flashscore.com Free Live Scores & Odds

Flashscore is one of the fastest live-score platforms with broad league coverage and basic odds comparison for 1X2, over/under, and BTTS markets. It also offers predicted lineups pre-match.

Key Features: Lightning-fast live scores, basic odds from multiple bookmakers, predicted lineups, head-to-head stats, and league tables.

Best For: Live score tracking, quick odds comparison, basic match research.

Limitations: Shallow statistical depth, no advanced metrics, no aggregated player research.

9. SoccerSTATS

soccerstats.com Free Goal Timing Data

SoccerSTATS is a long-running stats aggregator with a unique strength: detailed goal-timing breakdowns. It shows exactly when teams score and concede in 15-minute intervals, which is invaluable for in-play betting.

Key Features: Goal timing charts (0-15, 16-30, etc.), league table splits, form comparisons, and head-to-head stats.

Best For: In-play goal markets,"next team to score" betting, time-based analysis.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Find teams that score heavily in the last 15 minutes → back them for late goals
  • Identify teams that concede early → fade them in first half handicap markets

Limitations: Rough interface, heavy ads, no advanced metrics, mobile-unfriendly.

10. The Analyst

theanalyst.com Free Stats Perform / Opta

The Analyst is Stats Perform's consumer-facing brand, powered by official Opta data. It publishes power rankings, predictions, and detailed advanced player/team stats with a polished visual interface.

Key Features: Match-by-match player stats, power rankings, predictive models, editorial analysis, advanced player comparison tools.

Best For: Editorial context around the numbers, Premier League and Champions League analysis.

Limitations: No betting odds, limited league coverage, slower content cadence.

11. 365Scores

365scores.com Free Global Coverage

365Scores offers broad worldwide league coverage with a clean live-score interface. Useful as a fallback for competitions the bigger platforms do not cover as well.

Key Features: Live scores, league tables, basic match stats, and broad competition coverage including niche leagues.

Best For: Following lesser-known leagues, fallback for niche competitions.

Limitations: UI behind SofaScore and FotMob, no aggregated player history, heavy ads.

12. Forebet

forebet.com Free Algorithmic Predictions

Forebet publishes math-based predictions for 1X2, over/under, BTTS, and correct score markets with a numerical confidence score. Covers most major leagues worldwide.

Key Features: Algorithmic match predictions, confidence ratings, league standings, and form tables.

Best For: Quick algorithmic prediction lookup.

Limitations: Closed model (no transparency), no advanced metrics, no player data, no odds comparison.

13. FotMob

fotmob.com Free Clean Mobile Experience

FotMob has the cleanest mobile interface among live-score apps and significantly fewer ads than SofaScore. It offers heatmaps, shot maps, xG breakdowns, and reliable injury/suspension data.

Key Features: Live scores, xG breakdowns, heatmaps, shot maps, push notifications, injury and suspension data, predicted lineups.

Best For: Mobile-first users on top European leagues.

Limitations: Fewer niche leagues than SofaScore, no distribution context on player stats (averages only).

14. Football-Data.co.uk

football-data.co.uk Free Historical CSV Data

Football-Data.co.uk looks like a website from 2000 because it is. But it holds the most-referenced free archive of historical match data in CSV format, including bookmaker odds, covering 20+ years for major leagues.

Key Features: CSV downloads with match results, half-time scores, shots, corners, fouls, cards, and historical bookmaker odds from bet365, Betfair, Pinnacle, and others.

Best For: Building your own prediction models, back-testing strategies.

Limitations: No API, no UI, no live data, no advanced metrics.

Best Premium Football Statistics Platforms

15. Wyscout

wyscout.com From €150/month

Wyscout is the professional standard for football data and video analysis. Used by clubs, scouts, and professional analysts worldwide. Every touch of every player in major leagues is catalogued and searchable.

Key Features: Full match videos, individual player compilations, searchable by action type, detailed passing networks, defensive actions by zone, progressive plays, ball carrying, set piece analysis, and player comparison tools.

Best For: Professional bettors, syndicates, serious analysts with budget.

Limitations: Very expensive, steep learning curve, overkill for most individual bettors.

16. InStat

instatscout.com Custom Pricing

InStat offers AI-powered video tagging and tactical analysis across 50,000+ matches per year. Includes physical metrics (distance, sprints), technical actions, and team shape visualisation.

Best For: Syndicates, professional betting teams, and detailed scouting analysis.

Limitations: Professional-tier pricing, designed primarily for scouting rather than betting.

17. SmartScout & Betting-Specific Analytics

€50-100/month

Several betting-focused analytics platforms offer value-bet identification, predictive models (Poisson-based), odds comparison integration, and portfolio tracking. These include SmartScout and similar services.

Key Features: Algorithmic value-bet finders, expected value calculations, odds arbitrage identification, match outcome probabilities, bankroll management tools.

Best For: Serious bettors who want model-driven picks without building their own.

Limitations: Some models are"black boxes," quality varies by platform, requires trusting third-party algorithms.

18. Driblab

driblab.com Custom Pricing

Driblab is a professional football analytics platform with proprietary models including space distribution metrics, bypassing metrics, and player impact modelling. Used by clubs and agencies for recruitment and tactical analysis.

Best For: Advanced tactical analysis, player projection, and squad planning insights.

Limitations: Professional pricing, not directly designed for betting.

Odds Comparison & Betting Tools

19. OddsPortal / OddsChecker

oddsportal.com Free Odds Comparison

OddsPortal (also known as OddsChecker in some markets) aggregates odds from hundreds of bookmakers worldwide. It also provides historical odds data and odds movement tracking.

Key Features: Live odds from 50+ bookmakers, odds movement graphs, best odds guaranteed, historical odds archives, and match statistics alongside odds.

Best For: Finding the best price on any market, tracking odds movement.

How to Use for Betting:

  • Always check OddsPortal before placing a bet → the best price significantly impacts long-term ROI
  • Use odds movement graphs to see if the market is moving towards or away from a team
  • Compare odds across Asian bookmakers (Pinnacle, Singbet) for sharper lines

20. Betfair Exchange

betfair.com Free Exchange Trading

Betfair Exchange is the world's largest betting exchange. The odds here are often considered the"true" market price because they are set by users rather than bookmakers. Exchange data is invaluable for assessing market sentiment.

Key Features: Back and lay betting, matched volume data, market depth, historical exchange data, and API access for automated trading.

Best For: Understanding true market odds, laying teams, in-play trading, matched betting.

21. KickForm

kickform.com Free Data-Driven Predictions

KickForm uses a data-driven formula to provide match predictions, probabilities, and recommended tips for Europe's top 5 leagues. It is free and covers a wide range of markets including 1X2, BTTS, over/under, and correct score.

Best For: Quick, data-driven match previews with prediction probabilities.

How to Build a Statistics-Based Betting Strategy

Choose Your Platform Stack

Free Stack (Recommended for most bettors):

  • Primary: FBref (for deep advanced stats and xG)
  • Secondary: Understat (for xG visualisation and xPoints)
  • Mobile: SofaScore (for live updates, lineups, notifications)
  • Odds: OddsPortal (to find the best price)

Premium Stack (For serious bettors with budget):

  • Primary: Wyscout or InStat (professional-grade data)
  • Supplement: FBref for quick reference checks
  • Betting Engine: SmartScout or similar value-bet finder
  • Odds: Betfair Exchange API for automated price monitoring

Weekly Research Routine

Monday-Tuesday: Plan the Week
  • Use Soccerway to review all upcoming fixtures
  • Identify 10-15 matches with interesting statistical angles
  • Create a watchlist
Wednesday-Thursday: Deep Analysis
  • FBref: Check xG/xGA for watchlist teams → identify over/under performers
  • Understat: Review xPoints gaps → find value opportunities
  • WhoScored: Read match previews, check team strengths & weaknesses
  • Note initial betting ideas with expected value calculations
Friday-Saturday: Finalise Bets
  • Check latest team news (Transfermarkt, SofaScore lineups)
  • Review odds on OddsPortal → ensure best price
  • Calculate EV using your probability vs bookmaker implied probability
  • Place bets on clear value opportunities (EV > 5%)
  • Set SofaScore notifications for lineup confirmations
Sunday: Review & Learn
  • Analyse won and lost bets against the statistics
  • Did the data support the outcome? Record lessons learned
  • Update your model with new match data

Common Mistakes Using Football Statistics

Mistake 1: Data Overload Paralysis

Analysing 50 metrics per match leads to paralysis. Focus on 5-6 key metrics: xG, xGA, xPoints, recent form, home/away splits, and one market-specific stat (e.g., BTTS frequency).

Mistake 2: Ignoring Sample Size

A team's xG over 3 matches is unreliable noise. Require a minimum of 8-10 matches before drawing conclusions about a team's true performance level.

Mistake 3: Using Full-Season Stats in February

Teams evolve. A January transfer window signing, a new manager, or a tactical shift makes August stats irrelevant. Weight recent form (last 6-8 matches) more heavily.

Mistake 4: Trusting a Single Source

Different providers use different data collection methods. Cross-reference xG from FBref and Understat. Check odds across 3+ bookmakers on OddsPortal.

Mistake 5: Statistics Without Context

A team with strong xG but playing their 4th match in 12 days is not the same betting proposition as a fresh team. Stats measure capability, not situation.

Mistake 6: Betting Every Statistical Edge

If you find 20 value bets per week, you are over-trading. Be selective. Only bet when your expected value exceeds 5% and the sample size supports the conclusion.

Mistake 7: Ignoring Odds Movement

Your statistical edge may have already been priced into the market by the time you bet. Check current odds, not the opening price you saw on Monday.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free football statistics website?

FBref is the best free football statistics website for advanced analytics, offering xG, xA, progressive passes, and defensive metrics across 40+ leagues powered by StatsBomb data. For xG visualisation, Understat is the clearest option. For mobile live scores, SofaScore is the top choice.

Where can I find Expected Goals (xG) statistics for free?

The best free sources are FBref (broadest league coverage), Understat (best visualisation, top 5 leagues), WhoScored (match-level xG), and SofaScore (live xG during matches).

What is the best football statistics website for betting?

For direct betting research, FootyStats offers the best hit-rate tables for over/under, BTTS, and corners. FBref is best for identifying value through xG differentials. OddsPortal is essential for finding the best price once you have made your selection.

Which football statistics websites do professional bettors use?

Professional bettors typically combine FBref (advanced stats), Understat (xG visualisation), Transfermarkt (injury/squad data), Football-Data.co.uk (CSV downloads for modelling), a live scores app (SofaScore or FotMob), and Betfair Exchange (market sentiment). Those with budgets add Wyscout or InStat for video analysis.

Is FBref better than WhoScored?

They serve different purposes. FBref is better for deep statistical analysis, historical data, and advanced metrics (xG, progressive passes, pressures). WhoScored is better for match previews, live statistics, player ratings, and tactical analysis. Most serious bettors use both.

What statistics are most important for football betting?

The five most important metrics are: 1) Expected Goals (xG), 2) Expected Goals Against (xGA), 3) Expected Points (xPoints), 4) Home/Away form splits, and 5) Recent form (last 6-8 matches). These five metrics identify teams over/under-performing their true quality and reveal value betting opportunities.

How do I use xG statistics for betting?

Step 1: Compare a team's actual goals scored to their xG. If goals > xG consistently, regression is coming. Step 2: Compare goals conceded to xGA. If conceded < xGA, the defence is"lucky" and will regress. Step 3: Check xPoints on Understat. Teams below their xPoints line represent value. Step 4: Bet on teams underperforming their underlying numbers before the market corrects.

How often should I check football statistics?

For regular weekly betting, check statistics 2-3 times per week: Monday/Tuesday to review results and update data, Wednesday/Thursday for deep analysis of upcoming fixtures, and Friday/Saturday to finalise bets with latest team news.

What statistics should I track for BTTS betting?

Track these metrics: 1) Each team's BTTS percentage (FootyStats), 2) Both teams' xG and xGA (high on both sides = likely BTTS), 3) Clean sheet percentages (low = good BTTS candidate), 4) Head-to-head BTTS history, and 5) Home/away BTTS splits.

Are premium football statistics websites worth the cost?

Premium platforms like Wyscout (€150/month) and InStat (custom pricing) are worth it only for professional bettors with high monthly turnover (£5,000+). For casual and most serious bettors, free platforms (FBref, Understat, SofaScore) provide 90% of the useful data. The edge comes from how consistently you apply the data, not how much you pay for it.

How do I identify value bets using statistics?

Calculate your probability estimate using xG and form data. Convert bookmaker odds to implied probability. If your estimated probability is higher than the bookmaker's implied probability, you have found value. Only bet when your edge exceeds 5% to account for margin of error.

Can I use statistics for live betting?

Yes. Live xG is more reliable than the current scoreline. Use SofaScore for real-time xG, momentum graphs, and shot maps. Key live strategies: back teams dominating xG but losing (they are likely to score), and fade teams holding a lucky lead (opponent has higher xG).

What is the best way to learn football statistics for betting?

Start with FBref's beginner guides on xG. Follow analytics experts on Twitter/X (@MarkStats, @xGPhilosophy). Read our xG betting strategy guide. Track your bets in a spreadsheet noting the statistical reasoning behind each one. Review monthly to see what works. Focus on one league initially to build depth.

Which football stats site covers the most leagues?

SofaScore and Flashscore have the widest live coverage including obscure lower-tier and reserve competitions. FootyStats covers 2,000+ leagues for betting-specific stats. Soccerway also has extensive fixture coverage across hundreds of leagues worldwide.

Where can I get historical football data for building my own model?

Football-Data.co.uk provides free CSV downloads with match results, shots, corners, cards, and historical bookmaker odds going back 20+ years for major leagues. For xG data, FBref offers downloadable tables with full advanced metrics.
