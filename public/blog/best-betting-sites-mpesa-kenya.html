How to Deposit Using M-Pesa on Betting Sites

The process is the same across virtually all Kenyan betting sites:

  1. Log in to your betting account and go to the deposit section
  2. Select M-Pesa as your payment method
  3. Enter the amount you want to deposit (minimum KES 50-100 typically)
  4. You will receive an M-Pesa prompt on your phone asking you to enter your PIN
  5. Enter your PIN and confirm the payment
  6. Funds appear in your betting account instantly

For withdrawals, go to the withdrawal section, select M-Pesa, enter the amount, and the funds will be sent to your registered M-Pesa number.
