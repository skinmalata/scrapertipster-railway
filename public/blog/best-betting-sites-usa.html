Legal Status of Soccer Betting in the USA

Understanding the legal landscape is essential before you start betting. The United States has no federal online sports betting law - each state sets its own rules, creates its own regulatory body, and decides which sportsbooks can operate within its borders. This patchwork system creates a situation where a sportsbook that is fully legal and licensed in New Jersey might be completely unavailable in Texas.

The End of PASPA: The Game-Changer of 2018

Prior to 2018, the Professional and Amateur Sports Protection Act (PASPA) of 1992 effectively banned sports betting nationwide, with only a handful of exceptions (notably Nevada). That changed on May 14, 2018, when the Supreme Court ruled in Murphy v. NCAA that PASPA was unconstitutional. This decision gave each state the authority to legalize and regulate sports betting as they saw fit.

Since that landmark ruling, the growth has been exponential:

  • 38 states plus Washington, D.C. have legalized sports betting as of May 2026
  • 30+ states have active online/mobile sports betting
  • Annual sports betting handle exceeds $100 billion nationwide
  • Soccer is the 4th most bet sport in the US, behind football, basketball, and baseball
  • The 2026 World Cup is projected to generate over $2 billion in betting handle in the US alone

Important: Geolocation is Mandatory

All US-licensed sportsbooks use sophisticated geolocation technology to verify you are physically located within a state where they are licensed. You cannot bet from a state where the sportsbook does not operate, even if you have an account registered in a different state. VPN usage to bypass geolocation is a violation of every sportsbook's terms of service and may violate state laws. Your account can be closed and funds confiscated if detected.

Tribal Gaming Compacts

Many states operate under a dual system of commercial casino sportsbooks and tribal gaming. Federally recognized Native American tribes have sovereignty over gaming operations on their lands under the Indian Gaming Regulatory Act (IGRA) of 1988. In some states like Arizona, California, Oklahoma, and Florida, tribal gaming represents the primary or only legal sports betting option.

Points Betting: A Unique Product

What makes PointsBet different from every other sportsbook is"points betting," a form of wagering where your winnings or losses are not fixed at the time you place the bet, but instead vary based on how correct or incorrect your pick is. This is a concept that originated with spread betting in the UK and is fundamentally different from fixed-odds betting. Here is how it works for soccer: Suppose you bet"over 2.5 goals" on a fixed-odds sportsbook at -110. You bet $100, and if the match has 3+ goals, you win approximately $91. If it has 0-2 goals, you lose $100. Fixed outcome. On PointsBet, that same"over 2.5 goals" bet using points betting might be priced at 2.5 as the line. For every goal beyond 2.5, you win a multiple of your stake. For every goal below 2.5, you lose a multiple. If the match ends 4-0 (4 goals = 1.5 above the line), you win 1.5x your bet unit. If the match ends 5-0, you win 2.5x. If it ends 0-0 (2.5 below the line), you lose 2.5x your unit. PointsBet allows you to set"stop-loss" limits to cap your maximum potential loss, making this high-variance form of betting more manageable.

Line Shopping: The Most Important Habit for Profitable Betting

Regardless of which sportsbook you choose as your primary account, the single most profitable habit you can develop is line shopping. This means having accounts at 2-3 different sportsbooks and comparing the odds before placing every bet. The difference in odds between sportsbooks on identical markets is often enough to turn a long-term losing bettor into a long-term winning bettor.

Here is a real example from our testing: On a Premier League match between Manchester City and Arsenal, we found these moneyline odds across the top books:

  • FanDuel: Manchester City -125, Arsenal +310, Draw +270
  • DraftKings: Manchester City -130, Arsenal +300, Draw +260
  • Caesars: Manchester City -120, Arsenal +320, Draw +280
  • BetMGM: Manchester City -128, Arsenal +305, Draw +265

If you wanted to bet on Arsenal at +300 on DraftKings, that is the equivalent of 4.00 decimal odds. But if you line shopped and bet on Caesars at +320, that is 4.20 decimal odds. That 5% difference in odds directly translates to 5% better returns on winning bets. Over hundreds of bets, this compounds dramatically. To put this in perspective: a bettor with a 55% win rate betting at average odds of +100 (2.00) would have a 10% long-term ROI. That same bettor getting 5% better odds would have a 15.75% ROI - nearly 60% improvement. This is why line shopping is not optional for serious bettors; it is the foundation of profitable sports betting.
