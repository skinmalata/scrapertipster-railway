Kelly Criterion Explained: Optimal Staking for Soccer Bets

Updated April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team | 14 min read

The Kelly Criterion is a mathematically proven bankroll management system used by professional bettors worldwide. It tells you exactly how much to stake based on your edge and the odds available.

The Kelly Formula

Stake % = (B × P - Q) / B
Where: B = odds - 1, P = your probability estimate, Q = 1 - P

Simple Version

Stake % = (Odds × Probability) - 1

Example: Odds 3.00, Your probability 40% (0.40)

Kelly % = (3.00 × 0.40) - 1 = 1.2 - 1 = 20% of bankroll

How Kelly Works in Practice

Let's say your bankroll is £1,000 and you find a value bet:

  • Odds: 2.50
  • Your estimated probability: 45%
  • Kelly stake: (2.50 × 0.45) - 1 = 0.125 = 12.5%
  • Actual stake: £125

The Kelly Fraction: Half-Kelly & Quarter-Kelly

Full Kelly is extremely aggressive. Most bettors use fractional versions:

  • Full Kelly - Maximum growth, highest variance
  • Half-Kelly - 75% of growth with 50% variance
  • Quarter-Kelly - Conservative, smooth growth
💡 Recommendation: Start with Quarter-Kelly (divide result by 4) until you build confidence in your probability estimates.

Why Kelly Criterion Works

  • Maximizes growth - Mathematically optimal for bankroll expansion
  • Automatically adjusts - Bet size matches confidence
  • Protects bankroll - Never bets more than your edge warrants
  • Psychology-friendly - Removes emotion from stake decisions

Problems with Kelly

⚠️ Important Warnings:
  • Requires accurate probabilities - Bad estimates = disastrous results
  • Extreme variance - Full Kelly can halve your bankroll
  • Not for beginners - You need a proven betting edge first
  • Requires large bankroll - Handle the swings

How to Use Kelly Effectively

  1. Develop accurate probability estimates (at least 55% true accuracy)
  2. Only bet when you have positive expected value
  3. Start with Quarter-Kelly for safety
  4. Track your results over 200+ bets
  5. Adjust fraction based on confidence and variance tolerance

Verdict: Is Kelly Right for You?

The Kelly Criterion is the optimal staking method for serious bettors who:

  • ✅ Have a proven ability to estimate probabilities
  • ✅ Have enough bankroll to handle variance
  • ✅ Are patient for long-term growth
  • ✅ Can handle psychological pressure of large bets

If you're a beginner, start with flat staking and build your skills before attempting Kelly.
