5 Tips for Faster Withdrawals

  1. Complete KYC verification early. Most withdrawal delays happen because your account is not fully verified. Upload your ID and proof of address immediately after signing up.
  2. Use the same method for deposits and withdrawals. Most sites process withdrawals faster when you use the same payment method you deposited with.
  3. Betway, SportyBet, and Betika process M-Pesa withdrawals faster than bank transfers in Kenya. Choose M-Pesa whenever possible.
  4. Withdraw during business hours. Bank transfers initiated after 6 PM or on weekends may not process until the next business day.
  5. Check minimum withdrawal amounts. If you win small amounts, you may need to build up to the minimum withdrawal threshold before cashing out.
