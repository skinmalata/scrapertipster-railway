Asian Handicap +1.5: Underdog Value Strategy

Asian Handicap +1.5 gives the underdog 1.5 goal head start. You win if they lose by 1 or win/draw. Great value on competitive underdogs.

When to Back +1.5

  • Small underdog in competitive match
  • When you think they can keep it close
  • Home underdog vs top team
💡 Tip: +1.5 offers great value when underdog has strong home record.

Pinnacle and Sbobet offer best Asian Handicap +1.5 odds.
