Best Betting Sites in Germany 2026: GGL-Licensed Bookmakers Ranked

Germany liberalised online sports betting legally in 2021 and built a genuine regulated market around the GGL, the joint state gaming authority. Licensed bookmakers pay a 5.3% stake tax, players face deposit caps, and self-exclusion is centralised through OASIS. Here are the best of the legal options.

Germany is the largest sports-betting market in Europe, and since the Fourth Interstate Treaty on Gambling came into force in 2021, online sports betting has been fully legal there under a multi-licence regime watched over by the GGL, the Joint Gambling Authority of the States. For German bettors the result is a crowded, well-regulated market: every legal bookmaker pays a 5.3% stake tax, deposits are capped, and problem gamblers are flagged through a single national self-exclusion file called OASIS. This guide ranks the best licensed sportsbooks for 2026.

Know the Rules: GlüStV 2021 and the GGL

The legal basis is the State Treaty on Gambling (Glücksspielstaatsvertrag 2021), in force since 1 July 2021. It created an orderly online market: sports betting is legal and licensed, virtual slot machines and online poker are legal under GGL licences, and online table games remain largely restricted to a few state-level licences. The GGL, based in Halle, Saxony-Anhalt, has run licensing and enforcement since the start of 2023.

The restrictions matter for everyday players. Online slots are capped at a stake per spin (the cap was raised above euro 1 from July 2026 after years of debate and heavy criticism), there is a five-second delay between spins, deposits across each operator are limited to a default euro 1,000 a month extendable by verified income, and progressive jackpots are banned. None of that applies to sports betting in the old casino sense, but the deposit cap and OASIS cover your sports betting accounts too.

Tax: The 5.3% Stake Tax and Player Winnings

Germany taxes the stake, not the winnings of recreational players. Sports betting and horse-race betting attract a 5.3% tax on stakes under the amended Rennwett- und Lotteriegesetz (effective about 5.03% of a bet once calculated from the base), and the same levy applies to virtual slots and online poker stakes where it bites hardest. The important consequence for players: what you win from licensed bookmakers is tax-free, but every bet you place is slightly more expensive than the posted odds imply, so it pays to think in terms of net value. Unlicensed operators sidestep the tax and are therefore cheaper - and that is exactly why the GGL chases them with payment blocking and IP blocking.

Payments for German Bettors

German bookmakers stick to conventional rails because the treaty requires euro transactions and bans crypto. Expect Skrill, Neteller, PayPal, Paysafecard and direct bank transfer on almost every licensed site, alongside Visa and Mastercard. Giro and SOFORT bank-instant methods are also common. The GGL's payment and IP blocking means unlicensed brands increasingly cannot even complete a deposit, so a licensed operator is effectively the only reliable option for a German account holder.

Bonuses and the Fine Print in 2026

Licensed German bookmakers compete hard on welcome offers - typically a matched deposit bet with wagering requirements in the 20-30 times range, usable on Bundesliga and best-of-live markets. Read the terms before leaping: the deposit cap means you cannot simply reload past euro 1,000 a month without verification, and stake tax is not part of the bonus calculation on either side. Treat each bonus as a small, time-limited value, not a reason to deposit more than your normal bankroll.

Responsible Gambling in Germany

Germany has the strongest mandatory player-protection toolkit in Europe's licensed markets: OASIS automatically blocks excluded players across every licensed operator, deposit caps apply by default, and every licensed site carries mandatory prevention and stake-limit prompts. Set a monthly budget inside your cap, use the exclusion tools if your habits slide, and if gambling stops being entertainment, free help is available through the Federal Centre for Health Education, BZgA, which funds an anonymous national gambling helpline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is online sports betting legal in Germany?

Yes. Online sports betting has been legal since 1 July 2021 under the GlüStV 2021 treaty, with licensing and enforcement handled by the GGL. Only GGL-licensed or whitelisted operators can legally accept German bets.

Which bookmakers are licensed in Germany?

About 34 licensed sportsbook brands are active, including Tipico, bwin, bet365, Dazn Bet, Betway, Betano, NetBet and Unibet. Every legal operator appears on the GGL operator whitelist at gluecksspiel-behoerde.de.

Is there tax on betting winnings in Germany?

Recreational players pay no tax on winnings from licensed operators. Instead Germany levies a 5.3% tax on stakes for sports and horse-race bets, effectively about 5.03% per bet, plus the same levy on virtual slot and poker stakes.

How does the euro 1,000 deposit cap work?

The GlüStV sets a default deposit limit of euro 1,000 per month per operator for online gambling, extendable by a fully documented income confirmation. It applies across sports betting, virtual slots and online poker on each licensed site.

What is OASIS in German gambling?

OASIS is the central self-exclusion and blocking register. Players who exclude themselves are automatically blocked at every licensed German operator within around a day, and the same file is used by land-based venues.
