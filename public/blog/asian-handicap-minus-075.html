Asian Handicap -0.75 Explained: Split Line Strategy

Asian Handicap -0.75 is a split line. Half your stake at -0.5, half at -1. It offers more flexibility than standard -0.5. Here's how to use it.

How -0.75 Works

Team A -0.75 vs Team B @ 1.85

  • Win by 2+ → Full win
  • Win by 1 → Half win (at -0.5)
  • Draw/Loss → Full loss

When to Use -0.75

  • Strong home team vs weak away
  • When -0.5 odds are too low
  • When you want some draw protection
💡 Tip: Use -0.75 when you think team will win but not by much.
