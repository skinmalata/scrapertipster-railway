4. Mobile App Experience Winner: 1xBet

1xBet offers native apps for both Android and iOS. The Android app is available on the Google Play Store in some regions and as an APK download, while the iOS app is on the Apple App Store. The interface is feature-rich with live streaming, cash-out, bet builder, and detailed statistics all built in. It can feel overwhelming at first, but power users love the depth of features.

Bet9ja's app is Android-only and available as an APK download from their website. It's functional but feels dated compared to 1xBet. However, Bet9ja's mobile website works well as a progressive web app (PWA), which many Nigerian users prefer since it avoids downloading another app. The lack of a native iOS app is Bet9ja's biggest mobile weakness.

Our Take

1xBet wins for features and iOS availability. Bet9ja wins for simplicity. If you want a clean, no-fuss experience on Android, Bet9ja's mobile site is perfectly fine. If you want live streaming and advanced bet types, 1xBet is the way to go.

5. Payment Methods & Withdrawal Speed Winner: 1xBet

1xBet supports over 200 payment methods in Nigeria, including Opay, PalmPay, USSD banking, bank transfers, card payments, and cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, USDT, and more). This is by far the widest payment options of any betting site operating in Nigeria. Minimum deposit is ₦100, and withdrawals typically process within 1-24 hours.

Bet9ja supports around 15 payment methods: bank transfers, USSD, Quickteller, and card payments. They don't support crypto or mobile wallets like Opay and PalmPay directly. Minimum deposit is ₦100, and withdrawals take 2-24 hours. However, Bet9ja's 300+ retail shops let you deposit and withdraw cash in person, which is a major advantage for bettors who prefer dealing in physical cash.

Key Difference

  • 1xBet: Best for digital-native bettors who want crypto, Opay, PalmPay, and every payment option imaginable
  • Bet9ja: Best for cash bettors who want to walk into a shop and deposit or withdraw physically

6. NPFL Coverage & Local Markets Winner: Bet9ja

Bet9ja dominates local football coverage. They offer 80+ betting markets per NPFL match, including player props, corners, cards, and special markets that 1xBet simply doesn't cover. Bet9ja also has the most comprehensive virtual football offering in Nigeria, which is hugely popular with the local betting audience.

1xBet covers NPFL but with fewer markets (around 40 per match). They focus more on European leagues and international competitions. If NPFL betting is your primary focus, Bet9ja is clearly the better platform.

7. Live Betting & Streaming Winner: 1xBet

1xBet offers free live streaming on thousands of matches every month, including football, tennis, basketball, and more. Their live betting platform is one of the most comprehensive in the world, with real-time odds updates, live statistics, and a wide range of in-play markets. You can watch and bet on the same platform without switching apps.

Bet9ja's live betting is solid with fast odds updates, but they don't offer live streaming. Their cash-out feature is available on most markets, which is a plus. For the complete live betting experience with streaming, 1xBet is the clear winner.

8. Trust, Licensing & History Winner: Bet9ja

Bet9ja has been operating in Nigeria since 2013 and holds both LSLB (Lagos State Lotteries Board) and NLRC (National Lotteries Regulatory Commission) licences. They are the most recognised betting brand in Nigeria and have built trust through consistent payouts, regulatory compliance, and a massive retail network.

1xBet was founded in 2007 internationally and holds an NLRC licence to operate in Nigeria. They are a legitimate global operator, but they lack the long Nigeria-specific track record and dual licensing that Bet9ja has. When it comes to trusting a platform with your money in the Nigerian context, Bet9ja's local history gives it the edge.

9. Customer Support Winner: Draw

1xBet offers 24/7 live chat, email support, and phone support. Their live chat responds within minutes at any time of day, which is a significant advantage. Bet9ja offers phone support, email, and social media support. Their response times are generally good but can be slow during peak weekend hours when their retail shops are busiest.

Both platforms have adequate customer support. 1xBet's 24/7 live chat gives them a slight edge for immediacy, while Bet9ja's local presence means you can often resolve issues at a retail shop near you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which has a bigger welcome bonus, 1xBet or Bet9ja?
1xBet has a much bigger welcome bonus: 300% up to ₦1,200,000 compared to Bet9ja's 170% up to ₦100,000. However, Bet9ja's bonus terms are simpler, while 1xBet's wagering requirement (5x at odds 1.40+) is more achievable than Bet9ja's 10x at odds 3.0+.
Can I use Opay or PalmPay on Bet9ja?
No, Bet9ja does not directly support Opay or PalmPay. You can use bank transfers, USSD, and card payments. 1xBet supports Opay, PalmPay, USSD, bank transfers, cards, and cryptocurrency, making it the better choice for mobile wallet users.
Which app is better, 1xBet or Bet9ja?
1xBet has the better mobile app overall — it's available on both Android and iOS, includes free live streaming, and has more features. Bet9ja's app is Android-only and feels dated, but their mobile website works well as a PWA.
Does 1xBet have retail shops in Nigeria?
No, 1xBet operates purely online in Nigeria with no physical retail presence. Bet9ja has 300+ retail shops across the country where you can deposit, withdraw, and place bets in person.
Which site is better for NPFL betting?
Bet9ja is better for NPFL betting. They offer 80+ markets per NPFL match compared to 1xBet's 40+. Bet9ja also has deeper liquidity on Nigerian football, resulting in better odds for local league matches.
