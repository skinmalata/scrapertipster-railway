Cards Betting: Yellow & Red Card Markets Guide

Updated April 2026 | By WinFulltime Team | 10 min read

Tip: Looking for today's football predictions? Visit the homepage.

Cards betting offers unique opportunities. Bet on total cards, specific team cards, or booking points. Great for in-play.

Best Card Markets

  • Over/Under 3.5, 4.5, 5.5 cards
  • Booking points (10pts = yellow, 25 = red)
  • First card timing
  • Team to receive most cards
💡 Tip: Derby games and volatile matches have more cards.
