Why Corner Betting Has Value

Corner markets are less efficient than goal or result markets because:

  • Fewer bettors engage with them
  • Bookmakers set lines using basic averages rather than deep analysis
  • Corner data is freely available on SofaScore and Flashscore
  • Your research can realistically beat the bookmaker's crude corner estimates

Key Corner Markets Explained

Total Corners (Over/Under)

The most popular corner market. Bet on whether the total corners in a match will be over or under a set line (typically 9.5, 10.5, or 11.5).

Asian Total Corners

Asian Handicap-style corner lines. E.g., Over 10 corners (with the ability to push if exactly 10). Available on 1xBet and Asian-focused bookmakers.

Team-Specific Corners

Each team's corner count independently. E.g., "Arsenal over 6 corners." This is one of the most profitable markets for bettors who research team corner tendencies.

First Corner

Which team takes the first corner of the match? Low odds but researchable â€” teams that dominate early territory and create early attacks tend to win the first corner.

Corner Timing Markets

Over/Under corners in specific intervals: "Over 5 corners in first half," "Over 6 corners in second half." These are available on Bet365.

The Home Dominance Corner Strategy

Focus on matches where a dominant home team faces a defensive opponent. The home team will attack relentlessly, generating shots, saves, and corners while the opponent camps in their own half. A team like Man City vs a low-block side like Wolves can easily produce 12+ corners for Man City alone.

Check SofaScore's corner stats for both teams' last 5 matches, and the home team's home corner average.

Pro Tip: In-Play Corner Betting

After 25 minutes, check the corner count. If it's significantly below the line (e.g., 3 corners at 25' when the line is 10.5), back the Over for the full second half. Teams that haven't produced corners yet will press harder, and the line becomes biased toward the Under. Back the Over in the second half at inflated odds. SportsbookReview's live betting data shows second-half corners are significantly undervalued by in-play markets.

Corner Betting Mistakes to Avoid

Using total match line without splitting: First and second half corners often average differently. Use half-by-half research rather than assuming uniform distribution.

Ignoring goalkeeper quality: A world-class goalkeeper makes more saves and prevents more corners (goals saved often result in corners). A poor goalkeeper parries shots instead of catching them â€” leading to more corners for the opposition.

Over-betting on low-liquidity markets: Corner lines for lower leagues are less accurately priced, but also have higher variance. Use smaller stakes on obscure leagues.

The Bottom Line

Corner betting rewards disciplined, data-driven research. The market inefficiency means the gap between your research and the bookmaker's line is where your edge lives. Focus on team-specific corners, use SofaScore and FBref corner data, and target in-play second-half corners for maximum value.
