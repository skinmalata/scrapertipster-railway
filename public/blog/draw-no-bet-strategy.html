Draw No Bet Strategy: Reduce Risk and Lock in Profits

Draw No Bet (DNB) removes the draw from your bet. If your team draws, you get your stake back. It's a lower-risk alternative to 1X2 betting, perfect when you fancy a team but want protection.

How Draw No Bet Works

You bet on either team to win. If they win, you win. If they draw, you get stake back. If they lose, you lose.

Example: Liverpool vs Chelsea

Liverpool DNB @ 1.75

  • Liverpool win → You win (1.75 × stake)
  • Draw → Stake returned (no loss)
  • Liverpool lose → You lose stake

When to Use DNB

  • When you think team will win but draw possible
  • As Asian Handicap alternative
  • Accumulator building - reduces leg risk
  • When odds are close to Asian handicap
💡 Tip: Compare DNB odds to Asian Handicap - sometimes one offers better value.

DNB vs 1X2 vs Asian Handicap

DNB sits between 1X2 and Asian Handicap in risk. Good for matches where you're confident in a win but want protection.
