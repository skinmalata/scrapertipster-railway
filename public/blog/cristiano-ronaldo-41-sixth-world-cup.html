The Saudi Chapter

Ronaldo's move to Al-Nassr in January 2023 was widely viewed as the end of his elite career. But Ronaldo had other plans. In Saudi Arabia, he has been the most prolific version of himself since his prime at Real Madrid. In the 2024-25 season, at age 40, he scored 44 goals in 45 matches across all competitions. He was the Saudi Pro League's top scorer, the league's Player of the Season, and the leading scorer in the AFC Champions League.

The image of Ronaldo walking off the pitch in tears, having potentially played his last World Cup match, was one of the defining images of the 2022 tournament. Four years later, he is back.
