1xBet is one of the largest online betting platforms in the world, serving over 400,000 users across more than 100 countries. Founded in 2007 and headquartered in Cyprus, the company holds a Curaçao eGaming license (OGL/2024/1262/0493) and has expanded aggressively into African, Asian, and Latin American markets. But is it actually safe to use? And is it banned where you live? This review answers every question.

Company Background and Ownership

1xBet is operated by Caecus N.V., a company registered in Curaçao under the laws of the Curaçao Gaming Authority. The platform launched in 2007 as a Russian-facing bookmaker and has since grown into one of the world's largest online gambling operators. The company has partnerships with major football clubs including FC Barcelona, Serie A, and the Nigerian national football team, as reported by Reuters.

1xBet's rapid expansion has not been without controversy. The company has faced regulatory scrutiny in multiple jurisdictions, and its UK Gambling Commission license was revoked in 2019 following an investigation into the operator's compliance practices, as documented by the UK Gambling Commission. Despite this, 1xBet continues to operate legally under its Curaçao license and holds local licenses in over 35 countries across Africa, Latin America, and Asia.
