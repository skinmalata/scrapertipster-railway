How the Accumulator Builder Works

Our ticket builder algorithm scans all available predictions across 1X2, Over/Under, BTTS, corners, cards, and streak markets to find combinations of selections whose multiplied odds match your target.

  • Target Odds — The total accumulator odds you want to achieve (e.g., 20.00 for a 20/1 shot).
  • Min/Max Odds per Leg — Filters individual selections within your preferred odds range.
  • Only Today — Restrict to today's fixtures or include future matches for more combinations.

Each generated ticket shows the match, predicted outcome, individual odds, and total accumulator price. The algorithm prioritizes combinations closest to your target odds.

What markets are included in the accumulator builder?

The builder covers 1X2 (match result), Over 1.5 goals, Over 2.5 goals, BTTS Yes, BTTS No, and Double Chance selections derived from unbeaten streak analysis. All predictions are powered by AI analysis of team form, head-to-head records, and statistical models.

Can I filter accumulator tickets by odds range?

Yes. Set a target total odds, a minimum odds per leg, and a maximum odds per leg. The builder then finds the optimal combination of selections within your specified range.

What does double chance mean in an accumulator?

Double chance covers two of three possible match outcomes in a single selection. For example, "Home or Draw" wins if the home team wins or the match ends in a draw. This increases your probability of winning each leg, making it popular for accumulators.

How are the prediction odds calculated?

Odds are derived from AI-predicted win probabilities. The formula divides 100 by the probability and adjusts for a ~5% bookmaker margin. For example, a 70% probability translates to approximately 1.36 odds, giving realistic prices close to major bookmakers.
