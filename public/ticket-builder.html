Frequently Asked Questions

How does the football accumulator ticket builder work?

The accumulator ticket builder scans all available data-driven predictions across 1X2, Over/Under, BTTS, and double chance markets. It combines selections whose multiplied odds match your target, prioritising combinations closest to your desired accumulator price.

Is the accumulator ticket builder free?

Yes - the WinFulltime accumulator ticket builder is 100% free to use. No sign-ups, no subscriptions and no hidden fees. Build unlimited accumulator tickets every day.

What markets are included in the accumulator builder?

The builder covers 1X2 (match result), Over 1.5 goals, Over 2.5 goals, BTTS Yes, BTTS No, and Double Chance selections derived from unbeaten streak analysis. All predictions are based on statistical analysis of team form, head-to-head records, and models.

Can I filter accumulator tickets by odds range?

Yes. Set a target total odds, a minimum odds per leg, and a maximum odds per leg. The builder then finds the optimal combination of selections within your specified range.

Is the accumulator ticket builder free to use?

Yes, completely free. The builder supports up to 30 legs, target odds up to 500, market filtering, safe tips mode, and unlimited daily usage - with no account or payment required.

What does double chance mean in an accumulator?

Double chance covers two of three possible match outcomes in a single selection. For example, "Home or Draw" wins if the home team wins or the match ends in a draw. This increases your probability of winning each leg, making it popular for accumulators.

How are the prediction odds calculated?

Odds are derived from AI-predicted win probabilities. The formula divides 100 by the probability and adjusts for a ~5% bookmaker margin. For example, a 70% probability translates to approximately 1.36 odds, giving realistic prices close to major bookmakers.
