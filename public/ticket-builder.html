Frequently Asked Questions

How does SportyBuilder work?

SportyBuilder connects directly with SportyBet's active 24-hour sportsbook and cross-references each fixture with WinFulltime's statistical models (unbeaten streaks, H2H dominance, Over 1.5, and 1X2 win probabilities). It compiles the strongest combination matching your selected odds and registers a real booking code on SportyBet.

Why is the odds range restricted to 2.0 to 10.0?

Long-term accumulator profit relies on realistic probabilities. Limiting the range to 2.0–10.0 odds keeps your ticket composed of high-quality, data-backed selections rather than lottery bets with high failure rates.

Will I get the same matches every time I generate?

No. SportyBuilder contains an intelligent diversification engine with dynamic scoring jitter and duplicate penalization. Tapping 'Generate' or 'Shuffle' repeatedly automatically swaps in fresh, qualified fixtures.

Are these booking codes ready to bet immediately?

Yes. The generated booking code is registered directly on SportyBet. Simply copy the code, open your SportyBet app or website, tap 'Load Code', and your betslip will instantly populate with today's selections.
