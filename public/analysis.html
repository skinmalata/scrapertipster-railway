Football Match Analysis

What is Match Analysis?

WinFulltime provides detailed football match analysis to help you make informed betting decisions. Our analysis includes:

  • Recent Form - Last 5 match results for each team
  • Head-to-Head - Historical results between the two teams
  • Team Statistics - Goals scored, conceded, win rates, and more
  • Predictions - AI-generated tips with probability percentages

How to View Analysis

Click on any match from the home page to view detailed analysis for that specific fixture. Analysis is available for 1X2, Over/Under, and BTTS predictions.

Available Prediction Markets

1X2 Predictions

Home win, draw, or away win predictions with probability percentages.

Over/Under Goals

Predictions for matches with over 1.5, over 2.5, or over 3.5 goals.

BTTS Predictions

Both Teams To Score tips - whether both teams will find the net.

Corners & Cards

Specialized predictions for corner kicks and yellow/red cards.
