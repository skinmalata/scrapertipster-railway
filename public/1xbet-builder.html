Frequently Asked Questions

How does 1xBetBuilder generate 1xBet booking codes?

1xBetBuilder pulls live fixtures kicking off in the next 24 hours, aligns them with today's top 1X2 statistical models (unbeaten streaks, H2H dominance and win probabilities), and automatically registers a real, shareable 1xBet booking code.

What odds range can I generate?

1xBetBuilder generates accumulator tickets from 2.0 up to 50.0 odds using straight home and away win selections. Standard odds up to 9.5 are available to all users for free, while selecting 10 odds and above (10.0, 20.0 and 50.0 odds) is an exclusive WinFulltime Pro feature.

Will I get the same matches every time I generate?

No. 1xBetBuilder contains an intelligent diversification engine with dynamic scoring jitter and duplicate penalization. Tapping Generate or Shuffle repeatedly automatically swaps in fresh, qualified fixtures.

Are these booking codes ready to bet immediately?

Yes. The generated booking code is registered directly on 1xBet. Simply copy the code, open your 1xBet app or website, tap Load booking code, and your betslip will instantly populate with today's selections.

Can I convert a 1xBet booking code to SportyBet?

Yes. After generating a 1xBet code, tap Convert to SportyBet. The converter maps each 1X2 selection to its shared Sportradar match ID and creates a fresh SportyBet booking code from the same fixtures. If a match is not offered on SportyBet, the converter tells you which selection could not be mapped.

Is 1xBetBuilder free?

Yes. 1xBetBuilder is free for standard odds up to 9.5, with no sign-up or subscription required. Accumulators for 10 odds and above (10x, 20x and 50x) are unlocked with a WinFulltime Pro membership.
