Frequently Asked Questions

How are Best Picks selected?

Our AI scans every prediction across all markets (1X2, Over/Under, BTTS, Corners, Cards, Streaks) and picks the single selection with the highest confidence probability in each category.

What markets are covered?

Best Picks covers 1X2 (match result), Over 1.5 goals, Over 2.5 goals, BTTS Yes, BTTS No, Corners, Cards, To Score 2+, Win Streak, Loss Streak, and Draw Streak.

How is historical accuracy tracked?

Each day's top picks are compared against actual match results. A pick is marked Won when the outcome matches the prediction, Lost when it doesn't, or Pending if the match hasn't been played yet.

Are these picks guaranteed to win?

No prediction is guaranteed. Best Picks highlight the highest-confidence selections from our AI models, but all sports betting carries risk. Please gamble responsibly.
