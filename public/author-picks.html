Frequently Asked Questions

What are H2H Picks?

H2H Picks are WinFulltime's curated daily football predictions. Every fixture from today's calendar is analyzed across multiple markets — 1X2, Over/Under, BTTS, Draw No Bet, Corners, and Cards. The strongest independent selection per match becomes that fixture's Author Pick.

How is each pick determined?

Each fixture is evaluated using head-to-head history, recent team form, and matchup-specific statistical models. Markets compete on confidence score, and the highest-confidence selection per match is chosen. Only one market per fixture is used to avoid correlated picks.

What markets does H2H Picks cover?

H2H Picks scans Match Winner (1X2), Goals Over/Under, Both Teams to Score (BTTS), Draw No Bet (DNB), Corners, and Cards. The best-performing market for each individual fixture is selected based on historical and statistical strength.

How is historical accuracy tracked?

Each day's published picks are matched against actual match results the following day. Picks are marked Won, Lost, Push, or Pending depending on the outcome. The Performance section below shows win/loss records for recent days with full match details.

Is this feature free?

Free members get a curated preview of the day's H2H Picks. The full daily pool — every analyzed fixture with its market, tip and analysis — is a WinFulltime Pro feature. WinFulltime provides analytical information only and does not accept bets.

Are these predictions guaranteed?

No prediction is guaranteed. H2H Picks are based on statistical analysis and historical data, but all sports betting carries risk. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Please gamble responsibly.
