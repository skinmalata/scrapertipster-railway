Betting Options Guide

Learn about all the different football betting markets available on WinFulltime. From traditional 1X2 to more specialized markets like Corners and Cards.

1X2 Over 1.5 Over 2.5 BTTS Corners Cards To Score 2+ Streaks Double Chance Draw No Bet Halftime/Fulltime Correct Score First Goal Asian Handicap Odd/Even

1X2 1X2 (Match Result)

The most popular and straightforward betting market. You predict the final outcome of a match:

  • 1 (Home Win) — Bet on the home team to win
  • X (Draw) — Bet on the match ending in a draw
  • 2 (Away Win) — Bet on the away team to win
Example: Manchester United vs Liverpool

• 1 — Manchester United wins the match

• X — The match ends in a draw

• 2 — Liverpool wins the match

Tip: Research home and away form. Some teams perform significantly better at home.

O1.5 Over 1.5 Goals

A popular market where you bet on the total number of goals in a match being more than 1.5. This means at least 2 goals must be scored for your bet to win.

Example: Arsenal vs Chelsea (2-1)

• Total goals: 3

• Over 1.5 Goals: WIN (3 > 1.5)

• If the match ended 0-0 or 1-0, Over 1.5 would LOSE

Tip: Look for matches between teams with strong attacking records and poor defensive form.

O2.5 Over 2.5 Goals

Bet on the total goals in a match being more than 2.5. You need 3 or more goals for this bet to win. One of the most popular betting markets.

Example: Barcelona vs Real Madrid (3-2)

• Total goals: 5

• Over 2.5 Goals: WIN (5 > 2.5)

• If the match ended 1-1, Over 2.5 would LOSE

Tip: High-scoring leagues like the Premier League and Bundesliga are great for this market.

BTTS Both Teams To Score (BTTS)

A simple yes/no market where you bet on whether both teams will score at least one goal each during the match.

Example: Juventus vs AC Milan (2-2)

• Both teams scored: Yes

• BTTS Yes: WIN (Both teams scored)

• If the match ended 2-0, BTTS Yes would LOSE

Tip: Teams with leaky defenses and attacking strength are ideal for BTTS bets.

⛃ Over 9.5 Corners

Bet on the total number of corner kicks in a match being more than 9.5. This is a popular alternative market that doesn't depend on the match result.

Example: Bayern Munich vs Dortmund

• Total corners: 12

• Over 9.5 Corners: WIN (12 > 9.5)

• If total corners were 7, Over 9.5 would LOSE

Tip: Teams that dominate possession and attack down the wings typically win more corners.

📄 Over 4.5 Cards

Bet on the total number of yellow and red cards in a match being more than 4.5. This is a unique market that adds excitement to any match.

Example: Derby Match

• Total cards: 6 (5 yellow + 1 red)

• Over 4.5 Cards: WIN (6 > 4.5)

• If total cards were 3, Over 4.5 would LOSE

Tip: Local derbies and matches between rivals often have more cards. Check for aggressive teams.

🏆 Team To Score 2+ Goals

Bet on a specific team to score at least 2 goals in a match. This is great for backing strong attacking teams against weaker defenses.

Example: Manchester City vs Southampton

• Manchester City scores: 4 goals

• Man City Score 2+: WIN (4 ≥ 2)

• If Man City scored only 1 goal, the bet would LOSE

Tip: Look for teams in good form playing against teams with poor defensive records.

🔥 Team Streaks

WinFulltime tracks teams on winning, losing, and drawing streaks. This market helps you identify teams in exceptional form (or struggling).

  • Winning Streak: Teams that have won multiple consecutive matches
  • Losing Streak: Teams that have lost multiple consecutive matches
  • Drawing Streak: Teams that have drawn multiple consecutive matches
Example

• A team on a 5-match winning streak is likely in excellent form

• A team on a 5-match losing streak may continue to struggle

Tip: Consider the quality of opponents when evaluating streaks. A winning streak against weak teams may not continue against stronger opposition.

DC Double Chance

A lower-risk market where you can cover two of the three possible outcomes in a match. Your bet wins if either outcome occurs.

  • 1X — Home win or draw
  • X2 — Draw or away win
  • 12 — Home win or away win (no draw)
Example: Liverpool vs Everton

• 1X wins if Liverpool wins or draws (2 outcomes covered)

• X2 wins if Everton wins or draws

• 12 wins if either team wins (no draw)

DNB Draw No Bet (DNB)

You bet on either team to win. If the match ends in a draw, your stake is refunded. This removes the draw as a possible losing outcome.

Example: Chelsea vs Arsenal (1-1)

• You bet on Chelsea (DNB)

• Match ends in a draw: Stake refunded

• If Chelsea won, you'd WIN. If Arsenal won, you'd LOSE

HF Halftime/Fulltime

Predict both the halftime result AND the fulltime result. This is a higher-risk, higher-reward market.

Example: Barcelona vs Real Madrid

• 1/1 — Home team leads at halftime AND wins at fulltime

• X/2 — Draw at halftime, away team wins at fulltime

• 2/1 — Away team leads at halftime, home team wins at fulltime

If Barcelona was leading 2-0 at halftime and won 3-1: 1/1 wins

CS Correct Score

Predict the exact final score of the match. Higher odds but harder to win. Great for accumulator bets.

Example: Tottenham vs Newcastle

• You bet on 2-1

• Match ends 2-1: WIN

• Match ends 3-1, 2-0, or any other score: LOSE

FG First Goal Scorer

Bet on which player will score the first goal in the match. Great for adding excitement to matches.

Example: Man City vs Tottenham

• You bet on Haaland to score first

• Haaland scores in the 5th minute: WIN

• Any other player scores first, or no goals: LOSE

Tip: Check the team's penalty taker and set-piece takers. They're more likely to score first.

AH Asian Handicap

A sophisticated market where one team receives a goal advantage or disadvantage. Eliminates the draw option.

  • -1.0 — Team must win by 2+ goals
  • -0.5 — Team must win by 1+ goal
  • 0.0 — Draw refunds stake (similar to DNB)
  • +0.5 — Team can win, draw, or lose by 1
  • +1.0 — Team can win, draw, or lose by 1 (lose by 2+ loses)
Example: Team A (-1.0) vs Team B

• Team A wins 2-0: WIN (won by 2)

• Team A wins 1-0: WIN (by 1 = push, stake returned)

• Match ends draw: LOSE

OE Odd/Even Goals

Bet on whether the total number of goals in the match will be an odd or even number.

Example: Juventus vs Roma (3-2)

• Total goals: 5 (odd)

• Odd: WIN

• If match ended 2-2 (4 goals), Even would WIN

Tip: 0-0 results in an even number. This market is essentially a 50/50.

CS Clean Sheet

Bet on whether a team will keep a clean sheet (not concede any goals) in the match.

Example: Man City vs Wolves

• Bet on Man City Clean Sheet: Yes

• Man City wins 3-0: WIN (kept clean sheet)

• Man City wins 2-1: LOSE (conceded 1 goal)

WBH Win Both Halves

Bet on a team to win both the first half AND the second half of the match. Both conditions must be met for the bet to win.

Example: Liverpool vs Southampton

• First half: Liverpool 2-0

• Second half: Liverpool 1-0

• Win Both Halves: WIN

If second half was 0-1 (lost): LOSE

2H Second Half 1X2

Predict the result of only the second half of the match. The first half result doesn't matter.

Example: Match ends 2-2 (HT: 2-0 Home)

• Second half: 0-2 (Away wins second half)

• 2 (Away Win) for second half: WIN

B+O BTTS + Over 2.5 Goals

A combined market requiring both teams to score AND total goals to exceed 2.5. Higher odds due to both conditions needing to be met.

Example: Chelsea vs Arsenal (3-2)

• Both teams scored: Yes

• Total goals: 5 (>2.5)

• BTTS + Over 2.5: WIN

If match ended 1-0: LOSE

WTN Win to Nil

Bet on a team to win the match while keeping a clean sheet (not conceding any goals).

Example: Real Madrid vs Valencia

• Real Madrid wins 3-0: Win to Nil: WIN

• Real Madrid wins 2-1: LOSE (conceded)

TS2 Team to Score in 2nd Half

Bet on whether a specific team will score in the second half of the match.

Example: Bayern vs Dortmund

• First half: 0-0

• Second half: Bayern scores in the 60th minute

• Bayern Score 2nd Half: WIN

General Betting Tips

📈 Do Your Research

Always analyze team form, head-to-head records, injuries, and other factors before placing your bets. WinFulltime provides detailed statistics to help you make informed decisions.

💰 Manage Your Bankroll

Never bet more than you can afford to lose. A common strategy is to only stake 1-5% of your bankroll on any single bet.

🔴 Shop for the Best Odds

Different bookmakers offer different odds. Always check multiple sources to ensure you're getting the best value for your bets.

⏱ Avoid Emotional Betting

Don't bet on your favorite team just because you want them to win. Base your decisions on data and analysis, not emotions.

📚 Keep Records

Track your bets to understand what's working and what's not. This helps you improve your betting strategy over time.
