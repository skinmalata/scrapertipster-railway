About These Predictions

What does Over 2.5 goals mean?

Over 2.5 means the total goals scored by both teams must be 3 or more. Scores like 2-1, 3-0, 1-2, 2-2, or higher win. Scores of 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, or 1-1 lose.

How often do matches go over 2.5 goals?

Approximately 50-55% of professional football matches produce 3 or more goals. The rate varies significantly by league — the Eredivisie and Bundesliga consistently produce the highest Over 2.5 rates.

Is Over 2.5 the best goal market for accumulators?

Over 2.5 is the sweet spot for most bettors — it offers a good balance of odds (typically 1.70-2.10) and probability. Combined with 1X2 picks, it creates well-rounded accumulator tickets.

How do Over 2.5 predictions work?

Our model analyzes attacking strength, defensive vulnerability, head-to-head goal history, recent scoring form, and match context. Matches with probabilities above 70% are flagged as strong Over 2.5 candidates.

Which leagues produce the most Over 2.5 results?

The Eredivisie (Netherlands), Bundesliga (Germany), and Swiss Super League consistently produce the highest Over 2.5 rates. The Premier League and La Liga also tend to have above-average goal totals.
