About These Predictions

What does GG2+ mean in football betting?

GG2+ (Gol Gol 2+) means you expect both teams to score 2 or more goals each in the match. Winning scorelines include 2-2, 3-2, 2-3, 3-3 and anything else where each side nets at least twice.

How is GG2+ different from BTTS?

BTTS Yes only needs both teams to score at least once (a 1-1 wins). GG2+ needs each team to score at least twice (a 1-1 loses, a 2-2 wins), making it a far bigger price and a rarer outcome.

Does GG2+ require over 4.5 total goals?

Yes. Since each team must score 2+, the match always produces 4 or more goals in total. That makes Over 3.5 goals a strict prerequisite and Over 4.5 an implied requirement of every GG2+ ticket.

Are GG2+ tips good for accumulators?

GG2+ pays very high odds, so a single selection is often strong enough for a multi. We recommend pairing GG2+ picks with Over 3.5 goals or lower-stakes legs rather than stacking several GG2+ selections together.

Which matches suit GG2+?

High-scoring fixtures between two attacking teams with vulnerable defences — typically the Eredivisie, Bundesliga, or open South American matchups — suit GG2+ best. Our model looks for strong Over 3.5 and BTTS signals.

How are GG2+ probabilities calculated?

Using Statarea over-lines (Over 1.5/2.5/3.5) we fit a Poisson goal model, split expected goals between home and away from the 1X2 spread, then multiply P(home scores 2+) by P(away scores 2+). Only picks above a confidence threshold are published.
