Frequently Asked Questions

What is a player shots tip?

A player shots tip takes the players who shoot the most in the league, checks who is playing today, and maps their shots-per-game average to an over/under shots line. For example, a striker averaging 3.8 shots per game gets a tip of Over 3.5 shots in today's match.

How are player shots tips calculated?

We pull season shot totals for every player in Europe's top 5 leagues from Understat each day. For each of today's matches we rank both squads by shots per game (minimum 3 appearances and 1.5 shots per game), then suggest the highest whole-and-a-half shots line that sits below each player's average.

Is the shots line the same for every player?

No. The line is set just under each player's own shots-per-game average - for example, a player averaging 2.8 shots per game gets Over 2.5 shots. There is no fixed line for everyone.

Do I need to check the lineup before betting?

Yes. Always confirm the player is likely to start before placing a bet - rotation and injuries can change lineups on matchday.

What do the Top 20 tables show?

Two views of Europe's top 5 leagues: Top 20 Overall ranks players by total shots, and Top 20 per Match ranks them by shots per match, with matches played, goals and xG for each player.

How often is this page updated?

The data is refreshed once every day. Each entry shows the date it was last updated so you always know how current the numbers are.
