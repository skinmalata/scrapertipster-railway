About These Predictions

What counts as an unbeaten streak?

An unbeaten streak is a sequence of consecutive matches in which a team has not lost. This includes wins and draws. A streak ends when the team suffers a defeat. We track streaks of 5 or more games across all competitions.

How is an unbeaten streak different from a win streak?

A win streak only counts consecutive victories, while an unbeaten streak includes draws. A team can be unbeaten in 10 games but have drawn 4 of them. Unbeaten streaks are a broader measure of consistency.

Why do unbeaten streaks matter for betting?

Teams on long unbeaten runs signal tactical consistency and psychological confidence. Mid-table teams with quiet 8-12 game unbeaten runs often offer value odds. Unbeaten streaks help identify form that bookmakers may undervalue.

Can I combine unbeaten streaks with other markets?

Yes. Unbeaten streak data works well alongside 1X2 tips and Over 1.5 goals in accumulators. Teams on long unbeaten runs are statistically more likely to avoid defeat, making them strong double-chance or draw-no-bet picks.

How do I read the unbeaten streak badges?

Each match card shows the fixture, kick-off time, league, and streak badges. A badge reading "8 unbeaten" means the team has not lost in their last 8 matches. Some entries show "home" or "away" to indicate the streak applies to specific venues only.

When is unbeaten streak data updated?

Data updates daily. The primary update runs at 1:00 AM WAT and a secondary update at 6:00 AM WAT ensures late results and newly scheduled fixtures are captured across dozens of leagues worldwide.
