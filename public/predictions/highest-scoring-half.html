About These Predictions

What is a highest scoring half prediction?

A highest scoring half prediction asks a simple question: will the first half or the second half produce more goals in a match? Every pick on this page names the half we expect to outscore the other.

How are highest scoring half predictions calculated?

We take the expected goals for the match and split them across the two halves using a fitted first-half share. A Poisson race then compares the goals we expect before and after half-time. A pick only publishes when the winning half clears a 55% probability floor with a meaningful race margin, the match is a genuine goal game, and the model fit is solid.

What does the probability on a pick mean?

The probability is the model's chance that the named half outscores the other half in that match. A tie - equal goals in both halves - is a push and is never offered as a pick.

Which half usually scores more goals?

Historically around 44% of a match's goals arrive before half-time, so the second half edges it on average. Our picks highlight the fixtures where the goal split is lopsided enough to make a strong call in either direction.

When are highest scoring half predictions updated?

Predictions update daily alongside all other markets. The primary refresh runs at 1:00 AM WAT with a secondary update at 6:00 AM WAT to capture fixtures that appear closer to matchday.
