In-Play Betting Tips FAQ

How does this page work?

We analyse live football matches using real-time statistics, head-to-head records, and odds from API-Football. A tip only appears when a defined opportunity rule qualifies — for example, sustained attacking pressure in a goalless match. No tip is a valid result.

Why are there no tips?

Live tips only appear when a specific rule qualifies during an in-progress match. During off-peak hours or when matches don't meet the rules, the page will correctly show no opportunities.

How often do the tips update?

The live data refreshes every 15 minutes to stay within the API free-plan limit.

What do the odds and market mean?

The market is the specific bet type (e.g. Over 0.5 Match Goals) and the odds are the live price from a bookmaker. Odds change as the match progresses.
