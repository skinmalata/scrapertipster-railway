Last updated: March 2026

1. Our Commitment

WinFulltime is committed to producing accurate, transparent and independent football analysis and betting-related content. This Editorial Policy sets out the standards we apply to our predictions, articles, statistics and tips.

Our editorial team selects fixtures, markets and predictions on the basis of data and analysis alone. No bookmaker, advertiser or partner influences which picks we publish, how they are rated, or what is written in our guides and articles.

3. Accuracy and Sourcing

Where we reference statistics, records, odds or historical results, we make every effort to verify them against reliable sources, including official governing bodies, league data providers and bookmaker markets. Sports data changes quickly, and fixture lists, line-ups and odds can change without notice. We aim to keep our pages up to date, but we recommend readers confirm the latest information before acting on it.

If we publish an error, we correct it promptly and transparently. Where a factual mistake may have materially affected a page, we update the content and note the correction. To report an error, contact us through our Contact page with a link to the page in question.

We may use automated tools to assist with research, data processing or drafting. All published content is reviewed and approved by our editorial team, which remains responsible for everything that appears on this site.
