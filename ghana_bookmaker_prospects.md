## Regulatory note (important — read first)

- All licences below are for the GCG licensing period **1 Jan 2026 – 31 Dec 2026**, unless stated.
- GCG publishes trade name, operation type, dates and location only — **it does NOT publish operator domains or licence numbers**. Licence numbers in this report come from each operator's own site/footer and are marked where applicable. GHASBO (ghasbo.com/resources) mirrors the register.
- Sports-betting-related trade names from the GCG register total **~32** (types "Sports Betting", "Online Sports Betting", "Online & Land-Based Sports Betting"). All are listed below.
- **Do NOT prospect against these signatures — they are NOT on the 2026 GCG register:**
  - `africabet.site` (AfricaBet, GCG-2024-00237 — 2024 licence, not renewed for 2026; support@africabet.com belongs to that site).
  - BETBORO (was on 2025 list, absent from 2026 register).
  - `1win.com.gh` (1Win — not GCG-licensed; only offshore casino licences).
  - `m-portss.com`, `msportghana.org` (fake MSport clones; `support@msport.com.gh`, `+233 800 123 456` are FAKE).
  - `kbet.ng` / `kbet.ug` (Nigeria Winlot 888 Ltd / Uganda Kagwirawo KBET — different companies from the Ghana GCG "KBET" row).
  - `melbet-ghana.com` and similar `-ghana.com` review sites (content/affiliate sites, not operators).
  - The "KBET" Brazilian-lookalike spam pages (`kbetac.com`, `kbetl7.com`, `kebetam.com`) — EasyPaisa/BRL scam mirrors, avoid.
- Same-name different-entity caveats: `melbet.com.gh` is the official Ghana domain (OTRADA LIMITED); `alfabets.com` is the UK one (Ghana GCG "ALFABET" row is a different, locally-licensed entity); `afriluck.com` is the NLA-lottery brand (separate from GCG's AFRILUCKBET sportsbook).
- For any dispute about whether a brand is licensed, verify directly: GCG 027 469 8935 / info@gamingcommission.gov.gh.
- Where a public/verified marketing or partnership email is NOT known, the field below says “No publicly verified email found”. These operators typically require using the site's contact form, live chat, or phone.
